[Setup note to players] Setup (the role list itself is secret).
This game has 7 players. Its roles were drawn at random by the standard setup: usually 3 Yakuza for 13 players (about 3 in 13 seats; sometimes one fewer, occasionally one more); 0 to 3 Neutrals, usually 1 or 2; the rest are Good: at least 5 Village roles that aren't Outsiders (at least one of them Investigative), plus any mix of Village roles, up to 2 Vanilla Villagers and up to 2 Outsiders. Roles may repeat, except unique ones (R2). The roles are picked so that the sides are roughly balanced by strength: e.g. fewer Yakuza tend to get stronger roles.
