## Moderator strategy review (claude-opus-5.5)

The Yakuza (Carol and Frank, both Grunts) won even though Frank publicly claimed Grunt, a Yakuza-only role, on Day 2 and was hanged on Day 3. Three things decided the game.

1. **The Village lost its only reliable investigator early.** Heidi claimed Sheriff on Day 2, and Grace (Medic) protected Erin instead of her on Night 2. Heidi died after getting an Evil result on Frank, and her will didn't mention her Night 2 target, so the result was lost.

2. **Three of the Village were Roleplayers: Alice (believed Scout), Dave (Profiler) and Erin (Coroner).** All their results were random, and the town treated them as mechanical proof.
   - Erin's random "Frank was a Hitman" made people doubt a correct hang.
   - Alice's random visit groups were used to hang Bob (the real Vigilante) on Day 4 and Erin on Day 5. Both were Village.
   - Alice's results could not all be true. Her Night 2 claim of only 2 visit groups clashed with Grace's claimed visit to Erin. Her Night 4 claim that she and Grace were in separate groups clashed with Grace's will, which said Grace protected Alice that night. Nobody followed these clashes up, except Erin, too late.

3. **Carol played a clean low-profile game.** She claimed "Village, no night role" and was never made to name a role. She voted Guilty on her doomed teammate. She killed the claimed Sheriff, and after her attack on Alice failed she killed the protector (Grace). At 3 players she sided with Alice's false case to hang Erin, then killed Alice on Night 5.

Bob made it worse for the Village. He refused to name his role until he was on trial and never fired a shot, not even at the self-confessed Grunt.

- **deduction, for Village:** In hand-picked setups, assume Roleplayers may be in the game. Before using an investigative result to hang someone, check whether it is even possible given the visits other players have claimed. If an investigator's results contradict known facts or other claims, treat that investigator as possibly a Roleplayer, not as proof against whoever their elimination leaves. _(Alice's Scout results were random.
