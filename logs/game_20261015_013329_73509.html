Strategy reviewThe Village won in two nights because the Evil side kept giving away private information. On Night 1 the Yakuza chose a target with no lead and sent the Katana at Bob, who turned out to be the Serial Killer. His Standard Defense stopped it. That night Bob killed Alice (Lookout, Avenger), and her Avenger counterattack failed against the same Defense. Erin, Heidi, Frank and Bob all pressed her for a source. She called it "a guess from chatter" and was hanged 6-1. Carol, her Pharmacist teammate, rightly voted Yes and Guilty with the town and kept her own cover. Bob also made a fatal mistake: he publicly confirmed he had survived a Katana attack while claiming Coroner, a role with no Defense, and nobody had protected him. Erin took that as a sign of innate Defense and spent all 3 tokens on a Strong shot, which got through the Serial Killer's Standard Defense and killed him. The same night Carol blocked the Medic (Frank, who was protecting Heidi) and sent the Katana at Erin. Erin died, but her Avenger trait hit back at Carol, who had no Defense, and killed her. Bob's last kill landed on Dave, the Decoy, which gave Dave his win. That left only Frank and Heidi, so the Village won. What decided it: Grace's slip, Bob's public admission that he had survived, Erin spending enough tokens to beat the Defense, and an Avenger trait punishing the Yakuza kill.
