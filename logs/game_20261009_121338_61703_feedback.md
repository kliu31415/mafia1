## Outside review (claude-opus-5.5)

- **low, moderator error:** Players killed at night did not consistently get the R3 notice that they were attacked. Dave (N1), Mallory (N3) and Erin (N4) were told they were attacked with the Katana and died. Grace (N2) and Niaj (N6) got no such message. Alice (N7) was told only that her Katana attack succeeded, not that she was hit by a trap and died. No gameplay effect, since they were dead, but the handling is inconsistent.
- **low, moderator error:** Ivan's N2 notice said 'you handed Frank a philanthropy token'. Under the rules, a Philanthropist gives a token of the recipient's role kind, not a philanthropy token. The wording could mislead the Philanthropist about what was transferred.
- **low, harness bug:** The per-night 'Night actions' lists and the reveal leave out the Ex-Con's automatic random visit (Niaj). It appears only inside the moderator summaries. Several players (Alice, Bob, Erin, Heidi, Mallory, Carol, Niaj) flagged in feedback that this makes post-game checking harder.
- **low, harness bug:** Messages written by the Puppeteer are not marked as puppeted in the puppeted player's later history or in the reveal. Niaj and Olivia both reported this, and Niaj had to use his will to disown his Day 6 messages.
- **low, rules ambiguity:** The Fortune Teller rules don't say whether a self-targeted visit (e.g. Medic self-protect, which R6 says shows as visiting oneself) makes that player count as 'visited'. On Day 6, Alice and Bob argued that a self-protect plus the Katana would break 4&4, and Carol and Heidi argued the opposite. Several feedback entries called the self-visit wording ambiguous. Worked examples (self-visits, a Katana holder also using their own action) would help.
- **low, player confusion:** The FT numbers are unordered, but Judy's N3 result (3 and 4) was later read as '3 visitors on 4 targets'. That is impossible here, since nobody visited two players without being counted once as a visitor. The correct reading is 4 visitors on 3 targets. Ivan built part of his defense and will on the backwards reading, and Niaj's will pointed out the error.
- **low, rules ambiguity:** The Mortician text ('may spend a token to try to clean') leaves it slightly unclear whether the token is spent when the target survives. Alice lost a token on N5 when Heidi survived, and Frank flagged this in feedback as unclear.
