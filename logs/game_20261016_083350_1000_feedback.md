## Moderator strategy review (claude-opus-5.5)

The Village won even though it lost three of its own to hangings. The Yakuza (Erin the Fixer, Carol the Fabricator, Ivan and Mallory the Morticians) started strong. The same night, Bob spent a Basic shot on Ivan, whose Night 1 watch could not have been true, and killed a Mortician. On Day 4, Carol and Mallory each survived their trial 3-4. Two things saved them: Erin's Night 3 fix flipped Frank's Guilty vote, and Olivia, the Magpie, voted Innocent after Mallory exposed her. On Night 4 the Yakuza attacked Bob again. Frank's charm swallowed the Katana attack, and Bob's last shot killed Mallory, whose disguise read as Party Animal. On Day 5, Frank's swallow notice cleared him. Dave's Scout result ("only 2 groups: Bob | Dave") broke Carol's claim that she had tracked Dave. Carol was hanged 4-2 and the Village won.

The decisive plays were the Village protectors guarding the Vigilante, Bob's two Basic shots, and the Scout's group counts. The decisive Yakuza mistakes were fake results that broke the game's mechanics, and attacking the same protected target twice.

- **deduction, for everyone:** A Katana death means the holder visited the victim, and the holder reads Good to a Sheriff that night. If one claimant says X was the victim's only visitor and another says X read Evil, at least one of them is lying. Don't invent a rare trait to make both true and then hang the person they both accuse. Never fake a watch or track that leaves out a visitor who must have been there. Before you claim to have tracked someone, check whether a group count already shows that player alone. _(Ivan's watch left out the Katana holder, and that impossible result led Bob to shoot him. Carol's 'N4 tracked Dave, visited nobody' would have put Dave in a group with Carol, but Dave's result had him alone. That broken claim got Carol hanged and ended the game.)_
- **night actions, for Village protectors (Mirror Maker, Kitsune, Medic):** Near parity, protect the Village's night killer, the Vigilante. That player is the Yakuza's most predictable target. - **persuasion and voting, for Village:** When a claimant offers a public test that can't be faked and pays out the next morning (a Prosecutor's prosecution, a block the target will confirm), vote Innocent and take the test.
