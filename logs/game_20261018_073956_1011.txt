[Setup note to players] Setup (the role list itself is secret).
This game has 13 players. Its roles were drawn at random by the standard setup: 3 or 4 Yakuza for 13 players (each about half the time); 0 to 4 Neutrals, usually 2, at most 2 of them Neutral killers; the rest are Good: at least 5 Village roles that aren't Outsiders (at least 2 of them Investigative, 1 Killing and 1 Protective), plus any mix of Village roles and up to 2 Outsiders. Roles may repeat, except unique ones (R2). The roles are picked so that the sides are roughly balanced by strength: e.g. more Yakuza tend to get weaker roles. This setup never uses these roles, so nobody has them: Vanilla Villager, Grunt. Results that name a role (including fakes) never name them either. Separately, traits (R24) are dealt: each player, independently of everyone else, has 0, 1 or 2 traits, a 1/3 chance each (rarely fewer, when no other trait fits them). Which traits are dealt is drawn to help balance the sides; some traits can go only to Village players.
