## Outside review (claude-opus-5.5)

- **low, rules ambiguity:** Last wills can only be written before Night 1, at the start of each Day, or during Day turns, so a night-acting role that dies on Night 1 can't record that night's action in its will. Mallory (Party Animal) died N1 with the will 'N1: (target TBD)' and raised this in post-game feedback. The rules work as written, but this makes action-log wills useless on the first night.

Neither changed the outcome.
