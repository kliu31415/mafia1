Since the game starts with Night 1 and the first Day is Day 2, a literal reading gives no numbers on Day 2. The harness gave Carol her Night 1 numbers anyway, delivered at night and labelled 'read at the start of Day 2'. Ivan flagged in feedback that his night history was incomplete. Automatic-action notices should be consistent every night.

It never says when the fallback applies. It is also unclear whether 'the other players' excludes the profiled player. With 12 other players, Olivia treated the fakes as certain proof that Diviner and Wiretap were in play. That happened to be correct here, but the rules text appears to warn against exactly that inference.

- **low, other:** The moderator's Night 3 summary has internal annotations such as '(code op)' after actions ('Alice did a census around Ivan (code op)'). This is harmless because the summary isn't player-facing, but it suggests implementation notes are leaking into the moderator's output.
