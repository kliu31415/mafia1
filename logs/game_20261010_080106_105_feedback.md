- **high, harness bug:** Stated trial votes kept failing to match the revealed votes, always in the direction Guilty→Innocent, never the reverse. Day 3, Erin's trial: Grace, Ivan and Judy each publicly said they would vote Guilty but are recorded as Innocent. Day 3, Grace's trial: Ivan, Carol and Heidi (leaning) said Guilty but are recorded Innocent. It decided several trials. The harness should log when a vote is defaulted.
