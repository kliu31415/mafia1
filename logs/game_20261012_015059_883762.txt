[Setup note to players] Setup (the role list itself is secret).
This game has 9 players. Its roles were drawn at random by the standard setup: usually 3 Yakuza for 13 players (about 3 in 13 seats; sometimes one fewer, occasionally one more); 0 to 3 Neutrals, usually 1 or 2; the rest are Good: at least 5 Village roles that aren't Outsiders (at least 2 of them Investigative, 1 Killing and 1 Protective), plus any mix of Village roles and up to 2 Outsiders. Roles may repeat, except unique ones (R2). The roles are picked so that the sides are roughly balanced by strength: e.g. fewer Yakuza tend to get stronger roles. This setup never uses these roles, so nobody has them: Vanilla Villager, Grunt. Results that name a role (including fakes) never name them either. Separately, 0 to 3 traits (R24) are dealt to Village players, usually 1 or 2 (one player may get several), drawn to help balance the sides.
