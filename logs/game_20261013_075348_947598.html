Strategy reviewThe Village won on Day 3 by hanging the last Yakuza, Bob. The Yakuza lost mostly through their own visible play. On Day 1, Bob sent Grace a private message that everyone could see had been sent. That made them the only standout players, so on Night 1 three investigators targeted them: Carol tracked Bob, Erin checked Bob as Sheriff, and Dave watched Grace as Lookout. The Yakuza then skipped their Night 1 kill, which gave the Village a free round of checks. On Day 2, Bob was asked about eight times whether he had held the Katana (the Yakuza weapon, whose holder reads Not Evil to a Sheriff). He never gave a yes or no, and that refusal became the whole case against him. Frank, the Jester, nominated himself with no data. The town saw it as bait (Alice named it a likely Jester play), and the nomination got only Frank's own Yes vote. On Night 2 the Yakuza made two good moves: they killed Carol, the claimed Tracker, and Grace's Pharmacist block hit Erin, who was checking Grace that night. But Grace's Sleepwalker trait sent her on a random visit to Alice, a Veteran on alert, and the counterattack killed her. That left Bob alone. Dave's Lookout result (Carol and Grace visited Erin) matched Erin being role-blocked and Carol's Tracker claim. Erin then nominated Bob for his evasions. Bob's defense rested on the false idea that Grace could not have visited both Erin and Alice, which a Sleepwalker visit easily explains. He was hanged 5-1. Village mistakes: Carol publicly claimed Tracker on Day 2 and was killed that night. No one spelled out the strongest mechanical read: Erin was blocked and only Carol and Grace visited her, so Grace was the blocker and likely Yakuza.
