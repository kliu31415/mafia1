# What the players and reviewers said about the 12-player game

Sources: the post-game debriefs from 11 players (Erin's came back empty) and the moderator, the in-game feedback, the strategy lessons, the moderator's strategy review and the outside log review (`game_20261009_004910_895267_feedback.md`). I checked the claims that point at bugs against the JSONL log.

## The most important point: the bug invalidated parts of the game

The Azure refusal bug, which is now fixed, hit more than Ivan. **Judy's Night 1 Sheriff check was blocked too**: her night action shows `{'act': False}` after two refused calls. So her "Erin is Evil" result was invented: she believed she had a result and made one up. By coincidence it matched the truth, since an Executioner reads Evil. Ivan sat out nights 1 to 4, and the Yakuza's whole plan relied on his blocks. Almost every player built their analysis around these two players. **Treat this game's result and most of its strategy lessons with caution.** The players' points about mechanics and the interface still hold.

## Game mechanics and the interface

Ordered by how many players raised each point.

1. **A claims, results and votes tracker (raised by 8 players).** By days 4 to 6 the transcript was too long to track who claimed what, who got which result, who messaged whom, and who voted how. Players asked for a structured panel that keeps claims separate from confirmed facts.
2. **No log of a player's own night results (5).** Players couldn't easily look up their own past actions, results and tokens. Judy said she couldn't see her own N1 result, which makes sense because it didn't exist. Niaj couldn't reconstruct who he had doused. The moderator noted that some roles' results weren't logged on some nights, for example Grace on N1, N2 and N4.
3. **Last wills only update at the start of each day (5).** A claim made mid-day can't go into your will before you die that night. Frank's will said "Vanilla" after he claimed Medic, which exposed him. Mallory couldn't record a key private reply. Players asked for edits until the night begins.
4. **A night-by-night replay after the game (4).** Players wanted to see what actually happened each night: who held the Katana, frames, blocks, and failed attacks.
5. **Rules that are missing or unclear** (players and moderator):
   - **Lookout self-visits:** does a Lookout see their own visit? Mallory's "nobody visited Carol" result clashed with Dave's track, and nobody could resolve it.
   - **Medic feedback:** does a Medic learn when a protection stops an attack? Most of Day 3 was spent arguing about this. The moderator had no rule to point to.
   - **Executioner revenge targets:** can the pool include the Executioner, who has left the game, or dead players? The moderator chose living players only.
   - **What a frame does to Tracker, Lookout or Oracle:** the rule only covers alignment results.
   - **Katana with two Yakuza left:** one vote out of two isn't a majority, so one teammate who doesn't vote cancels the kill. The rules are correct here but don't spell it out. Bob asked for a revote, or a reminder to teammates who haven't voted.
   - **Fabricator:** can frame and forge target different players, and which field is which? (Frank)
   - **Silent-round day ending:** this is a program rule that isn't in the rules file. Mallory, Niaj and Carol said days ended right after an important private message, before anyone could react.
6. **Validating night actions (moderator).** Grace submitted Standard protection with tokens_to_spend=1. The moderator wanted the harness to flag that kind of contradiction, and wanted a structured view of the Yakuza's Katana votes before resolving the night.
7. **Repetitive players (5).** Dave and Grace repeated near-identical lines across many rounds, such as "tracking X tonight" and "I'll protect Y", instead of answering direct questions. Several players read that as a loop. Partly this is model behavior (both were Haiku), but players suggested a visible list of unanswered questions, or a prompt asking for new information or a pass.
8. **Cosmetic or unverified:** the winners list shows dead Village players as "winners", which is correct for a faction win but reads oddly. Bob said his prompt showed "Katana holder: None". The code gives the real holder (Frank) in the briefing, so this looks like a false memory, unless the prompt got lost because of the bug.

## Strategy

What decided the game, according to the moderator's review and the players:

- **Carol (GPT-Sol, Vigilante)** won it with three Basic shots, all on Yakuza. Each targeted a player whose votes contradicted their own stated reads: Frank voted No on the player he had accused, and Bob pushed to hang Heidi right after Frank flipped. Carol stayed hidden until her shots had confirmed her.
- **Grace (Haiku, Medic)** stopped the Katana on N1 and N2 with free Standard protection on likely targets.
- **Erin (Gemini Flash, Executioner)** won by claiming Sheriff early with an "Evil" result on her target. Judy's delayed counterclaim looked like it was made up after the fact. The town hanged Judy 8–4, even though Frank and Carol raised the Executioner possibility. (Judy's counterclaim really was made up, because of the bug.)
- **Niaj (Grok, Arsonist)** doused five times and never ignited. Two of his doused players died anyway. He made no case at three players left and was hanged 2–1.

Lessons that several players and the reviewer agreed on:

- **Lead with your results immediately**, and keep your will in sync with your public claim (Judy, Frank, Mallory, moderator).
- **Remember that Executioners and Jesters read Evil.** In a 1-v-1 Sheriff dispute on Day 2, consider a neutral pushing a hang before voting Guilty (Erin, Ivan, Judy, Mallory, moderator).
- **A visit shows a destination, not an action or alignment.** Tracking a Medic claimant can't separate a protection from an attack (Carol, Grace, Mallory).
- **Keep votes consistent with your reads.** Mismatches between what someone says and how they vote are what Vigilantes punish (Frank, Bob, Carol, moderator).
- **Yakuza should plan around a teammate who might not act.** This came from the bug, but it's a real weakness of the 2-person Katana vote.
- **Don't fake-claim a role that a living info role can contradict** (Frank, Bob, Alice).
- **Keep neutral killers in the suspect pool** after the Yakuza are gone (Carol, Bob).

## Suggested next steps

- **Quick rules fixes (yours):** Lookout self-visits, Medic feedback, Executioner revenge targets, frames against non-alignment roles, and the silent-round ending. Each could be one new rule paragraph (R16 and on).
- **Quick code changes:**
  - show each player a private history of their night actions and results in the prompt
  - allow will edits until night begins
  - add a structured Katana-vote view for the Yakuza, plus a warning when there's no majority
  - flag contradictory night submissions such as Standard protection with a token
- **Bigger change:** a claims, results and votes tracker in the prompt. 8 of 12 players asked for it.
- **Rerun** this setup now that the provider bug is fixed, for a clean read on Yakuza balance.
