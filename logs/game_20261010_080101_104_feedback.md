- **low, player confusion:** On Day 2 Judy claimed Heidi's Tracker claim 'cannot be true alongside my Seer claim'. Seer and Tracker can both exist, and the rules say roles may repeat, so there was no conflict. - **low, other:** The rules put no cap on failed nominations, so the only limit is speaking budget. Frank self-nominated more than a dozen times across Days 2–8, and Day 8 had a long run of repeated failed nominations. Nothing was actually broken; this is a design gap that lets nomination spam fill the Day. Grace's debrief remark about 'duplicated nomination entries' refers to these legitimate repeats, not to a logging bug.
- **low, prompt problem:** From Day 5 on, Grace repeatedly reported that her notepad's budget figure (3.41) didn't match the prompt (7.12–8.00). The notepad value was stale, and she used the prompt's figure each time. The prompt doesn't make clear that the notepad isn't updated with the authoritative budget, which caused confusion on every one of those turns. Frank's Day 8 and Grace's debrief feedback likewise say budget and carry-over are hard to track.
