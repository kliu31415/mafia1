This game has 8 players. Its roles were drawn at random by the standard setup: usually 3 Yakuza for 13 players (about 3 in 13 seats; sometimes one fewer, occasionally one more); 0 to 3 Neutrals, usually 1 or 2; the rest are Good: at least 5 Village roles that aren't Outsiders (at least 2 of them Investigative, 1 Killing and 1 Protective), plus any mix of Village roles and up to 2 Outsiders.
