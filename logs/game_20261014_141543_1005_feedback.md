## Moderator strategy review (claude-opus-5.5)

The Village won, but mostly because the Serial Killer did their work for them. Frank (Serial Killer) happened to kill all three Yakuza on Nights 1 to 3: Mallory, then Bob, then Grace. - **deduction, for Village:** Once a Fabricator is known to exist, treat a lone Evil Sheriff read as a lead, not a hang.
