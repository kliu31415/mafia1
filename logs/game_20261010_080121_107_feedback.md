## Moderator strategy review (claude-opus-5.5)

The Village lost by giving away its numbers on Day 2. Judy (Jester) self-nominated on Day 1 and openly invited the rope. Dave (Executioner on Judy) quietly built a 'vote-steering' case and nominated her. On Day 3 Bob's Lookout correctly caught Grace visiting Alice, and Grace even posted Diviner (a Yakuza-only) results, which outed her, and flatly denied the visit. - **deduction, for Oracle and other exact-role investigators:** If your result confirms a Jester or Executioner, reveal it publicly before the nomination vote, or at the latest in your trial speech. Protecting your role is worth far less than stopping a hang that hands Neutrals a win and triggers revenge kills. _(Carol knew Judy was the Jester after Night 1 but only voted Innocent with a vague 'a hanged Jester wins'. If you must hang, keep the Guilty side a bare majority so fewer Village are in the revenge pool. _(Judy self-nominated on Day 1 and on trial said 'I'd rather hang on a fair read'. Evil should press its advantage the moment the count allows. _(On Day 3 (6 alive, 3 Yakuza) Heidi, Ivan and Grace voted No and Bob's nomination of Grace failed at 3 Yes, despite strong evidence.
