## Lessons players wish they'd known

- **Alice (Jester, claude-haiku-5.5)**, persuasion and voting, for Jester or Executioner: A Jester wins by being hanged, so self-nominating and saying you won't object is read as a hang-seeking pattern. Make the push look like a reasoned vote on the record, and keep the nomination itself for the moment the table already leans toward you.
- **Alice (Jester, claude-haiku-5.5)**, deduction, for Village: A claimed investigative role that is killed by the Katana at night is probably the real one, since the Yakuza hit the threats they can't explain away. Use the night kill list as a claim test before voting on the claims themselves.
- **Alice (Jester, claude-haiku-5.5)**, night actions, for Yakuza: Keeping the Katana with one holder every night and hitting the most active claimant was never contested, because nobody could prove protection or a visit. Rotate the holder only when a Lookout or Tracker has named it.
- **Alice (Jester, claude-haiku-5.5)**, coordination, for Village: Repeated demands to name a night or a visitor, with no deadline, stalled Day 2 and Day 3 for several rounds while the Yakuza kept killing. Set one concrete question per round and vote on the answer, or nominate on the first clear contradiction.
- **Alice (Jester, claude-haiku-5.5)**, deception, for Yakuza: Once a Village player has claimed a role, have your teammates test that claim with a vote against it before it is confirmed. Their answers to direct questions then carry more weight than the claim itself.
