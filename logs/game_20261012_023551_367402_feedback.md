## Lessons players wish they'd known

- **Alice (Cartographer, claude-haiku-5.5)**, night actions, for Village investigators (Cartographer, Sheriff, Seer, Tracker, Lookout): Publish your mechanical result the moment it exists, with night, target and exact output. Don't defer it to 'the start of Day N'. A deferred result lets a duplicate claimant copy your numbers unchallenged, and it gives the Yakuza a free night to remove you before the result is ever read.
- **Alice (Cartographer, claude-haiku-5.5)**, deception, for Village players facing a duplicate claim: When someone copies a unique-sounding claim with identical numbers, don't both wait for the next night's test. Ask the copier for a concrete check that only the real holder could pass, and push for the nominee's mechanical contradiction instead of PM timing. Five PMs with a dead Yakuza was a lead, but the group sat on it for a full Day.
- **Alice (Cartographer, claude-haiku-5.5)**, coordination, for everyone: Don't let a Day pass with no nomination when a stall clock is running. Day 2 was spent saying 'wait for sizes' from every seat, and by Day 5 the Village had to choose between a weak hang and losing to R14. Set a concrete decision point (e.g., 'nominate on Day 3 if no mechanical result') and keep to it.
- **Alice (Cartographer, claude-haiku-5.5)**, rules and mechanics, for Medic and anyone relying on Standard protection: Standard Defense does not stop Strong attacks. Arsonist ignites and Serial Killers hit Strong, so a Medic who protects Standard on a likely Arsonist target has protected nothing. Use Strong protection on any player who may be doused or threatened by a Strong attacker, and keep one token for that.
- **Alice (Cartographer, claude-haiku-5.5)**, persuasion and voting, for Village players deciding a nomination: Don't hang on wording or timing alone. Two nominations this game (Frank over three changed answers, Heidi over watch targets posted after the nomination) were based on inconsistency, not a mechanical contradiction. Each cost a Village player or a wasted Day. Require one checkable result or one clear contradiction before voting Guilty, and state that rule publicly so others can hold you to it.
- **Bob (Fabricator, claude-haiku-5.5)**, coordination, for Yakuza: Don't claim a role that another living or dead player has already claimed with identical mechanical outputs. Copying Alice's Cartographer sizes exactly made my claim testable and it was immediately counterclaimed. Pick a role whose results can't be cross-checked against a public claim, or make the claim only after the real holder is dead and their results can't be contested.
- **Bob (Fabricator, claude-haiku-5.5)**, coordination, for Yakuza: Keep daytime teammate contact out of private chats. Five Day 2 PMs with my Katana-holding teammate were public as a count and became the town's main lead for the whole game. Do all coordination in night chat, and never let a teammate's daytime messages line up with yours.
- **Bob (Fabricator, claude-haiku-5.5)**, night actions, for Yakuza: Before a Katana attack on a Village claimant, remember that an Avenger trait makes a lone attacker die with the target. Carol's fatal Standard attack on Alice killed Carol through Avenger, which left the Yakuza with one player. Prefer a test (a frame, a Basic attack, or a delay) on a claimant whose death would cost the attacker.
- **Bob (Fabricator, claude-haiku-5.5)**, night actions, for Yakuza: Spend one-shot tokens (such as the Fabricator frame) while the holder is still alive and the frame can change a vote or a read. I died on Day 3 with my token unused, so the planned frame never happened.

## Outside review (claude-opus-5.5)

- **medium, harness bug:** The setup note says '0 to 3 traits (R24) are dealt to Village players, usually 1 or 2', but this game dealt five trait copies: Alice had Martyr, Armed and Avenger, Erin had Armed, and Grace had Armed. That is only 3 distinct trait types, so either the generator counts types rather than copies (and the setup text needs to say so) or it went over its own limit. Five traits also adds a lot of Village power in a game with only 2 Yakuza and 1 Neutral, which is at odds with 'drawn to help balance the sides'.
- **medium, player confusion:** Players often lost track of day and night numbers and asked for results from nights that hadn't happened. On Day 3 many players asked for 'Night 3 targets and results' before Night 3. On Day 5 Grace asked for 'any Night 5 result' and Frank said 'My Night 5 audit is set'. On Day 6 Dave asked for 'Night 6 douse, protection and watch targets'. Frank's mixed-up Night 3 status ('audit pending' / 'no audit submitted' / 'no action') then became the whole case for his Day 4 nomination. The prompt could state the phase clearly, e.g. 'Night N comes after Day N; results from the coming night arrive on Day N+1'.
- **low, player confusion:** On Day 6 Erin said Heidi's claimed Night 4 watch on Erin (no visitors) 'checks out with my own no-visit night'. Erin taking no action doesn't show whether anyone visited her, because a Lookout result is about visits to the watched player, so Erin treated the claim as confirmed when nothing confirmed it.
