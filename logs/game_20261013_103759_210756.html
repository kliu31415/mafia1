Moderator strategy review
Strategy reviewThe Village won on Night 2, and the Yakuza mostly lost it themselves. On Day 1 Carol (Sheriff) promised "concrete info tomorrow", which marked her as an investigator. Bob (Pharmacist, holding the Katana) blocked her and killed her with a Katana attack he made Strong using his Katana Expert trait. On Day 1 Dave (Grunt) had sent Bob a private message that everyone could see was sent. On Day 2 Bob claimed Tracker, which clashed with Frank, the real Tracker. Dave then sent Bob two more visible private messages. When asked what they said, Bob described them as advice to keep his Tracker explanation "concise and consistent". Dave confirmed it, which amounted to admitting he was coaching a fake claim. Frank nominated Dave, and Dave was hanged 4–3. The only Innocent votes came from Bob, Dave and Alice (Jester). Bob had argued for Dave and voted Innocent. Heidi (Vigilante) took that as her lead and spent all 3 tokens that night on a Strong shot that killed Bob. The Katana still killed Frank, because Bob had correctly blocked Erin, the Medic guarding him. But with both Yakuza dead, the Village won. The Village also handled Alice's repeated offers to be hanged well: they ignored her, so the Jester got nothing. The Yakuza also never used Dave's Katana Master, because the Katana never moved to him.
