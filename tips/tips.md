## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of every player's stated vote, the announced totals and your own vote. Ask for stated votes beforehand, flag any that shift or don't fit the totals, and vote as you said. (seen in 33 games)
- Totals can be rigged: a Ballot Stuffer adds a vote and a Fixer silently flips one player's trial votes. On a mismatch, first have everyone recheck their private reminder. Nomination counts can't be altered, so a mismatch there is a lie, possibly Jester bait. (seen in 5 games)
- Tokens die with you: spend one-shot tokens the first night they can swing a vote or remove a threat, not at a perfect moment. A puppet or doubled ballot lapses if you die that night. (seen in 23 games)
- Each Day, write past results and tonight's planned target in your will. The result from the night you die never reaches it, so post important results publicly while alive. (seen in 33 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action. A corroborated report of X as the lone unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof; from one unverified claimant it is only their word. (seen in 34 games)

## Village
- Investigators: post night, target and exact result once it bears on a vote; held-back results look retrofitted. But keep tonight's plans secret, even in PMs, and with no result a firm exact-role claim usually just draws the Katana or a Ritualist guess. (seen in 31 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name one corroborated mechanical fit: a broken claim, an Evil result against a Vanilla claim, elimination from a group result. One unverified claimant's late result naming their own accuser doesn't qualify. (seen in 45 games)
- Once a mechanical result exposes a Yakuza, suspect whoever defended them, delayed the hang or left an unexplained Innocent in the totals. PM links and proven lies are only leads: Jesters manufacture both. (seen in 35 games)
- Players who self-nominate, push without evidence or tell reckless checkable lies are often Jesters. Prefer hanging a likelier killer; otherwise keep Guilty to a bare majority, because revenge kills a random Guilty voter. (seen in 17 games)
- Hunt win-blocking Neutral killers (Serial Killer, Arsonist) deliberately: read every cause of death, list unwatched Vanilla claimants, unexplained survivors and visit-count gaps, and aim watches, audits and Oracle guesses there. Not Evil reads and quiet nights clear nobody. While the Katana still kills, hanging Jesters, Executioners, Magpies or spent Bounty Hunters wastes the Day. (seen in 17 games)
- Count toward vote lock each morning: once Yakuza are half the living, they can block every nomination. Hang your best suspect the Day before, even on a 50/50, rather than wait for tomorrow's test. (seen in 9 games)
- Cross group results (census, survey, map, Fortune Teller counts) with claimed visits, audits and confirmed roles to eliminate names; one unexplained visitor on a kill night is usually the Katana holder. (seen in 9 games)

## Yakuza
- Defending a doomed teammate rarely pays. Do the vote math in night chat; if you can't stop the hang, vote Guilty and say so, since totals get compared with stated votes. (seen in 46 games)
- Plan in night chat, not daytime PMs (the trail is public), and confirm the exact Katana holder and target nightly; a missing vote can cancel the kill. (seen in 40 games)
- Agree Village covers on Night 1 that fit your tokens and real visits, then keep them. Cornered switches, Yakuza-only role names and night-chat knowledge usually hang you; villagers should audit or Oracle-test fresh claims. (seen in 23 games)
- Give the Katana to a member whose cover explains visiting the victim, move it once a watch or count narrows on the holder, and admit an exposed visit rather than deny it. (seen in 22 games)
- Kill sources of future information (Census Taker, Cartographer, Fortune Teller, Coroner, blockers) before players whose results are already public; after a failed Katana, remove the protector rather than retry. (seen in 23 games)

## Coroner
- Examine bodies that settle a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim), not undisputed Katana victims. Post night, target and role the next Day, and keep tomorrow's exam secret or you're tonight's kill. (seen in 3 games)

## Medic
- Guard likely Katana targets such as claimed investigators, vary the pattern, tell nobody, and spend a token on self-protection soon after you claim. (seen in 27 games)

## Vigilante
- Dying with unused shots is the usual failure: set a trigger by Day 3 and fire on a solid lead rather than proof. (seen in 13 games)

## Smuggler
- Teammates shouldn't veto the swap: it moves only actions aimed at the two swapped players. Send the Katana at a decoy swapped with the Medic's likely ward, so the kill hits the ward, or swap yourself with a Villager to dodge a check. Both get notices, so prepare a cover. (seen in 11 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once three players are doused, or two if you're being questioned; each extra dousing night risks your hang. (seen in 12 games)
