# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-16 06:24 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 388 active entries of tips_notebook.json (95 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules faee28de8a58b5dc.

## Everyone
- Keep a notepad of each nomination's Yes count, each trial's totals and every stated vote. Ballots are secret, so this is the only record you have. A mismatch is usually a miscast, so ask the player once to re-check their reminder before you treat it as a lead. (seen in 22 games)
- When a total doesn't fit, ask voters to post what they cast and how it counted. Miscasts, lies and the Merciful or Zealot traits usually explain a gap before a Fixer does. A Fixer's flip repeats in every trial that Day. More votes than voters, after allowing for a revealed Mayor's double vote, means a Ballot Stuffer. (seen in 10 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens the first time they can swing a vote or remove a threat, because they die with you. Kill tokens are the exception: they tend to pay only on a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits. A claim clash, or a 'test' of an unverified claim, is not enough. At parity, fire anyway. (seen in 32 games)
- Check the rules before you call something a contradiction. Only the Mayor, Vulture, Hitman and Saint are unique, so test both claimants of a duplicate role. A visit shows a destination, not what the visitor did, and one player can visit several targets. (seen in 49 games)
- An unexplained Katana survival can come from a Medic, innate Defense (Serial Killer, Arsonist, Jester, Executioner), the hidden Narrow Escape trait (once per player) or a Paranoid trait's fake notice. It can also mean a hidden Shaky holder, whose Katana never kills. Failures on several targets with no protector usually point to Shaky. (seen in 27 games)
- Answer a question about your night action in one plain sentence the first time it is asked. Evasion repeated over several Days tends to become the case against you. (seen in 27 games)
- A Turncoat is a Village player who wins only if the Yakuza win. They read Evil to a Sheriff or Seer but Good to a census. Suspect a claimant who keeps protecting the main suspect and voting to save them, and count that player as a Yakuza vote. (seen in 2 games)

## Village
- Vote Guilty on a corroborated break. Examples: a claim of a Yakuza-only role (Grunt, Pharmacist, Diviner...), a first-person slip, a leak of night facts, a corroborated lone visitor to a victim, or a group result whose other members are confirmed. One unverified claimant's result is only a claim, since Roleplayers report random results. (seen in 76 games)
- Investigators: once a result bears on a vote, post the night, target and exact result, and keep your results and tonight's plan in your will. Don't announce tonight's target, even by PM. Leading the talk or hinting at a role on Days 1-2 without a result usually draws the Night 1 Katana. (seen in 50 games)
- Once mechanics show a player is Yakuza, look at who defended them, PM'd them or argued for delay. A lone Evil read doesn't show that: Jesters, Notorious and Turncoat players and framed players all read Evil. The Katana holder reads Not Evil. (seen in 53 games)
- Self-nominations, asking for the rope, or an Evil read with no claim behind it all fit a Jester. Usually leave them alive. If you hang them anyway, keep the Guilty votes to a bare majority. (seen in 27 games)
- Count down to the vote lock in effective votes: the Yakuza plus every vote sure to be Innocent. The Day before the lock is your last sure hang, so refuse 'one more night' and hang your best Yakuza lead. (seen in 14 games)
- Read every cause of death. A non-Katana attack cause proves that killer is alive, and a game that goes on after the Yakuza look dead means a win-blocking Neutral remains. List the unclaimed players, Vanilla claimants (a lie unless the setup's list names Vanilla) and unexplained survivors and visitors. Hang the best of them while the table is still large. (seen in 19 games)
- If you are Remorseful, vote Guilty only on corroborated cases, because a Village hang kills you too. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village.

## Yakuza
- Agree covers in Night 1 chat: each member gets a real Village role that fits their tokens and real visits, never Vanilla or a Yakuza-only role. Check every claimed visit against the posted logs, never speak from night-chat knowledge, and don't switch claims when cornered. (seen in 34 games)
- Don't defend a doomed teammate. Vote with the expected majority and state the vote you actually cast: hidden Innocents and Guilty minorities the size of your team both show in the totals. (seen in 64 games)
- Send no daytime PMs between teammates. Every member should submit the agreed holder and target every night: with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 71 games)
- Kill whoever's next result could expose you: a Census Taker, a counter, an exact-role checker or a blocker. On the night that brings parity, kill a Sniper or Vigilante with shots left. After a failed Katana attack, remove the likely protector rather than retrying. (seen in 38 games)

## Neutral
- Solo killers: settle early on one plain cover the setup allows, and invent no results anyone can check. Don't admit surviving an attack under a claim with no Defense, since that points to innate Defense. (seen in 8 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body whose role settles a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim) on the first night it exists. Post the night, target and role together. Your exam is Astral, so it stays your word alone until it fits other facts. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard the likeliest Katana targets, vary your pattern, keep your ward secret and never visit a claimed Veteran. Free Standard protection doesn't stop a Serial Killer's attack or an ignite, so switch to Strong tokens once one appears. (seen in 42 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once three players are doused, or two if you are being questioned. Each extra douse night is another visit that can be counted and another chance to be hanged first. (seen in 16 games)

## Jester
- On an early nomination Day, look like a real Yakuza and stick to one shaky claim. Begging for the rope or self-nominating again and again usually gets you refused. (seen in 9 games)
