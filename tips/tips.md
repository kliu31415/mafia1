# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-16 10:48 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 405 active entries of tips_notebook.json (98 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules 8f2138dae76eb0b8.

## Most often decisive
- Village: before voting Guilty, name one concrete break: a Yakuza-only role claim, a first-person slip about an attack, a knowledge leak, or a corroborated lone visitor whose claim doesn't fit. Check that the result behind it is itself corroborated. One unverified claimant's map, watch, count or profile may come from a Roleplayer or a liar, and such hangs have often hit Villagers. When one more night kill could hand the Yakuza the vote lock, hang the best case you have today. (seen in 78 games)
- Yakuza: avoid sending teammates private messages during the Day, above all on Day 1-2 and when a teammate is under pressure. Everyone sees that a PM was sent, and a Wiretap or Eavesdropper may read it. A visible PM triangle usually becomes the town's lead for the rest of the game. Plan in night chat. (seen in 72 games)
- Yakuza: don't defend a doomed teammate. The town compares the announced totals with stated votes, so a hidden Innocent shows up. Vote Guilty and say so, unless night-chat math shows your votes can actually save them. (seen in 66 games)
- When a reporter has independent support and X is the only other visitor (or the only unexplained one) to a Katana victim, treat it as near-proof. A visit report with no second source is only that player's word, especially one held back until the accused claimed or one that names the reporter's own accuser. (seen in 52 games)
- Village: tend not to announce tonight's target, or even that you will act or spend a token. It tells blockers and the Katana where to go. Once a result bears on a vote, post it in full: night, target and exact result. (seen in 52 games)

## Everyone
- Before each night, write your planned target in your will, with what each possible result would mean. A result from the night you die never reaches your will, so post important results publicly while you are alive. (seen in 55 games)
- Only the Mayor, Vulture, Hitman and Saint are Unique, and several games have had two real holders of the same role. Test both duplicate claimants (targets, exact wording, a watch or an exact-role check). Don't hang or shoot either one over the clash alone. (seen in 50 games)
- Keep a notepad of every nomination and trial: each player's stated vote, the announced totals and your own ballot. A stated vote that doesn't fit the totals is a lead, not proof. It is usually one miscast or misposted ballot, so ask that player once to re-check their reminder. Then check the plain causes: a lie (often from a Neutral or a Turncoat) or a Merciful or Zealot trait. Consider a Fixer or Ballot Stuffer only if something actually shows one exists. With a Fixer shown, one Guilty more than stated on a Village claimant's trial points at whoever pushed that hang. (seen in 38 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens the first Day or night they can change a vote or remove a threat, because they die with you. Kill tokens are the exception: they need a lead, meaning a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits. Don't fire at a claim clash, an untested claim or a Neutral that never blocks wins. Once the Yakuza hold half the votes, fire even on a coin flip. (seen in 34 games)
- Answer a direct question about your night in one plain sentence the first time it is asked, and never deny a visit that a visit result could expose. (seen in 29 games)
- A survived attack with no protector named can come from innate Defense (a Serial Killer or Arsonist), the hidden one-time Narrow Escape trait, a Shaky holder whose Katana never kills, or a fake notice from the hidden Paranoid trait. Failures on different targets fit one Shaky holder. Ask the survivor once for the exact notice. (seen in 28 games)
- Ask a concrete question once, by name. Save budget for trials and late rounds, and nominate in early rounds while nominations are still cheap. (seen in 27 games)

## Village
- Once a mechanical result shows a player is Yakuza, your next suspects are the players who argued for No or for delay, defended them, or traded PMs with them. Being caught lying, or reading Evil to a lone Sheriff, is not the same as being shown Yakuza: run the Jester check first. (seen in 54 games)
- On Day 1-2, don't hint at a power role and don't lead the talk while you have no result. The loudest organizer and fresh claimants are the usual Night 1 Katana targets. (seen in 42 games)
- Jester signs include self-nominating, asking for the rope, a Yes on their own nomination, and reckless claims with nothing behind them. Prefer to leave such a player alive. If you must hang one, keep Guilty to a bare majority and have confirmed power roles vote Innocent. (seen in 28 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers from Day 2. Read every cause of death, and keep a list of unclaimed or unwatched players, unexplained survivors and unexplained gaps in visit counts. A Not Evil read doesn't clear them. Near the vote lock, give the hang to your best Yakuza lead, because hanging a Neutral killer doesn't lower the Yakuza count. (seen in 21 games)
- Count down to the vote lock in effective votes: the Yakuza, plus the accused's own vote, declared Merciful voters and likely Turncoats. A Turncoat reads Evil but counts as Good in a census. Suspect one in a Village claimant who keeps protecting the main suspect and voting to save them. The Day before the lock is your last real hang, so turn down 'one more night of checks' on that Day. (seen in 15 games)
- (Remorseful) A Guilty vote on a hanged Villager kills you as well. Vote Guilty only on a corroborated case, and claim the trait before the vote. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village.

## Yakuza
- Confirm the holder and target in night chat every night, and make sure every member submits their vote. With two Yakuza alive, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 43 games)
- After a failed attack, don't retry blindly. Kill or block the protector, or move the Katana to a member with Katana Expert or Katana Master. A second failure on the same unprotected target points to real Defense, so push that player to the gallows. (seen in 41 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow down your team: a Census Taker first, then counting and group roles, then exact-role checkers. Expect fresh claimants and the loudest Day 1 player to be guarded. (seen in 39 games)
- Agree each member's cover on Night 1. Use a real Village role that fits your tokens and your actual visits, never Vanilla Villager or a Yakuza-only role, and check it against visit results already posted. If a claim breaks, don't switch to another: fallback claims usually get you hanged. Agree your public wording too, and never speak from your hidden perspective ('my Katana', who held it, who was blocked). (seen in 34 games)

## Sheriff
- A Not Evil read can mean that player held the Katana that night, so rank never-checked players above them. Once only one Yakuza is left, a Not Evil read clears nobody. Post an Evil read as 'reads Evil', not 'is Yakuza': Jesters, other Chaos Neutrals, Notorious or Turncoat Villagers and framed players also read Evil. (seen in 29 games)

## Coroner
- Examine a body whose role settles a live question, such as one side of a duplicate claim or a hanged suspect, on the first night it exists. Post the night, target and role in one message. A cleaned body gives you nothing. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Protect the likeliest Katana targets, such as claimed investigators, and don't visit a likely Veteran. Use Strong protection once a Strong attacker shows up, and self-protect the night after you claim. Dying with tokens wastes them. (seen in 42 games)

## Vigilante
- Set a trigger by Day 2-3 and fire a Basic shot on a real break. A Basic shot that fails with no protector named suggests innate Defense or Narrow Escape, so retry with 2 tokens. Post the role your kill reveals. (seen in 29 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once 3 or more living players are doused, or sooner if you are being questioned. Douses get lost to other deaths, and Arsonists who waited usually got hanged first. (seen in 17 games)
