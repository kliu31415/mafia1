# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-13 12:54 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 375 active entries of tips_notebook.json (82 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules bd1d10f9d243cd1c.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret: keep a notepad of stated votes, announced totals and your own vote, and vote as you said you would. A mismatch is usually one miscast ballot: a lead, rarely a hang. (seen in 38 games)
- When totals don't fit stated votes, first ask voters to re-check their reminders: a miscast or lie is far likelier than a Fixer or Ballot Stuffer. If your own vote seems flipped, say so and switch it next trial. (seen in 9 games)
- Tokens die with you: spend one-shot tokens the first time they can swing a vote or remove a threat. Village kill tokens instead need a lead (a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits), not a claim clash; at parity, fire anyway. (seen in 23 games)
- One unexplained Katana survival proves little: a hidden Shaky holder never kills, and hidden Narrow Escape saves anyone once. Read innate Defense only from repeat failures; Yakuza should move the Katana off a member who keeps failing.
- A visit shows a destination, not an action or alignment. A corroborated lone unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof; one unverified claimant's report is only their word. (seen in 43 games)
- Only Smuggler and Vulture are Unique, so a duplicate claim doesn't prove a liar, nor does it support either claimant: test both rather than accepting both. (seen in 43 games)
- Each night, put past results and tonight's plan in your will. The result of the night you die never reaches it, so post important results while alive. (seen in 48 games)

## Village
- Hunt Neutral killers deliberately: read every cause of death and list quiet, unclaimed or unexplained-survivor players (Not Evil doesn't clear them). But hanging one leaves the Yakuza count unchanged, so count the vote lock first; never-blocks-wins Neutrals rarely merit a hang. (seen in 13 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name a corroborated mechanical fit (a Yakuza-only role claim, a broken claim, an attack slip), not leans or wording. That decides whom to hang, not whether: with no result coming, hang the best lead. (seen in 64 games)
- Count down to the vote lock: once Yakuza hold half the living players, they vote down every nomination. 'Wait for tonight's results' is a common Yakuza line. (seen in 18 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows someone Yakuza, look at whoever argued delay, defended them or PM'd them. A merely caught liar may be a Jester, so don't chain from that. (seen in 47 games)
- Investigators and protectors: avoid announcing tonight's target or hinting at your role early, which guides blockers and the Katana. Once a result bears on a vote, post night, target and exact result promptly, will updated first. (seen in 46 games)
- A player who self-nominates or pushes without data is often a Jester. Prefer sparing them; if you hang, keep Guilty votes to a bare majority, since revenge hits a Guilty voter. (seen in 25 games)
- Alignment reads mislead: the Katana holder, Serial Killers and Arsonists read Not Evil, while Jesters, Executioners and Notorious or Turncoat Villagers read Evil. A Turncoat wins with the Yakuza but counts Good in a census; hanging one doesn't cut the Yakuza count. (seen in 25 games)
- If Remorseful, a Guilty vote on a hanged true Villager kills you, so vote Guilty only on corroborated cases. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village: recheck who pushed that case.

## Yakuza
- Keep team plans in night chat: daytime teammate PMs are visible to everyone and often become the town's only lead. (seen in 60 games)
- Don't defend a doomed teammate: totals expose a stray Innocent. Do the vote math in night chat; unless your votes can stop the hang, vote Guilty and say so. (seen in 56 games)
- Confirm Katana holder and target in night chat and all submit it; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 41 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1: a Village role fitting your tokens and visits, not a Yakuza-only role or Vanilla (normally not in play). A cornered claim switch is usually fatal. (seen in 30 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow your team (Census Taker, exact-role checkers, visit counters), not whoever already posted. After a failed Katana, deal with the likely protector before retrying. (seen in 32 games)
- Spend rigs, puppets, cleans and swaps on the first Day they can swing a vote; holders who wait usually die with them. (seen in 26 games)

## Coroner
- Examine bodies that settle a live question (a duplicate claim, a hanged suspect, a possibly forged will) on the first night one exists, then post night, target and role. Avoid announcing targets; cleaned bodies show nothing. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Free Standard protection on the likeliest Katana target (vocal analysts, then claimed investigators) often carries the town; spend a token on Strong once a Neutral killer shows. Keep wards secret. (seen in 34 games)

## Smuggler
- A swap moves exactly the actions aimed at the two swapped players, so plan it, don't veto it: aim the Katana at a decoy swapped with the Medic's likely ward, or swap a suspected teammate with a Villager to shed checks and shots. (seen in 14 games)
