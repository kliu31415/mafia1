# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-13 00:31 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 349 active entries of tips_notebook.json (76 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so note every stated vote and announced total, and vote as you said you would; a mismatch is usually a lead to question, rarely grounds to hang. (seen in 17 games)
- When a trial total doesn't fit stated votes, a miscast or misstated ballot is likeliest: ask voters once to re-check their reminders before suspecting a Fixer (flips one player's votes) or Ballot Stuffer (total exceeds voters). (seen in 8 games)
- Tokens die with you, so spend one-shot tokens the first time they can swing a vote or remove a threat. Kill shots are the exception: wait for a lead, not a bare claim clash, and skip Neutrals that never block wins. (seen in 19 games)
- Keep past results and tonight's plan in your will; the result of the night you die never reaches it, so post important results while alive. (seen in 42 games)
- Check the rules before calling a contradiction: only Smuggler and Vulture are Unique, so duplicate claimants are often both real, and a block or swap can explain a missing or odd result. (seen in 42 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action. A lone unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof only when corroborated; one unverified log is just a claim. (seen in 40 games)

## Village
- Convict on corroborated mechanical cases (a broken claim, an attack slip, a lone unexplained visitor) rather than wording or leans; when no result is coming, hang the best lead available. (seen in 59 games)
- Count the vote lock each morning: Yakuza holding half the living block every nomination, so 'wait for tonight's results' usually just costs an investigator. (seen in 15 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers deliberately: read every cause of death, and list quiet late-Vanilla players and unexplained attack survivors as suspects (Not Evil doesn't clear a Serial Killer or Arsonist). Near the lock, hang the Yakuza lead first. (seen in 12 games)
- Investigators: post night, target and exact result once it bears on a vote; held-back results tend to look retrofitted. (seen in 35 games)
- Avoid hinting at a power role on Day 1-2 or announcing tonight's target, even by PM (a Wiretap may read it): announced actors are usually that night's kill or block. (seen in 43 games)
- Once mechanics show a Yakuza, look at who delayed their hang, defended them or PM'd with them; a mere liar or lone Evil read may be a Jester. (seen in 43 games)
- Someone who self-nominates or pushes without data while reading Evil is often a Jester: prefer leaving them alive, or hang with a bare Guilty majority. (seen in 21 games)
- A public claim of a Yakuza-only role (Clerk, Wiretap, Diviner, Talent Agent...) is effectively a confession, even if retracted; hang that Day. (seen in 10 games)

## Yakuza
- Defending a doomed teammate rarely pays: totals expose stray Innocents, so unless your votes can stop the hang, vote Guilty and say so. (seen in 53 games)
- Keep plans in night chat; daytime PMs between teammates are visible to all and often become the town's main lead. (seen in 56 games)
- Confirm holder and target nightly and submit every Katana vote; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 38 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1 that fit your tokens and real visits, then keep them: audits, exact-role checks and visit counts break fakes, and a switched claim usually hangs you. (seen in 29 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would expose you (Census Taker, Oracle, Accountant, visit counters); at parity, a Vigilante or Sniper with shots comes first. (seen in 28 games)
- After a failed Katana, assume a protector or innate Defense; remove the likely Medic or strike elsewhere rather than retrying. (seen in 31 games)
- Give the Katana to an expendable member and move it once a watcher or count narrows the holder; fresh claimants are often guarded, trapped or watched. (seen in 25 games)

## Neutral
- Solo killers: take a stance and one plain, consistent claim early, and deny being attacked; silent late-Vanilla players are the easy hang. (seen in 6 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil, so a clean read clears no one outright; an Evil read also fits a Jester, Executioner, Ex-Con or frame. (seen in 23 games)

## Coroner
- Examine bodies that settle a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim) on the first night possible, post the result next Day, and don't announce your next exam. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard likely Katana targets (claimed investigators, vocal analysts) unannounced; Standard doesn't stop a Serial Killer or ignite, so spend a token on Strong once one is shown. (seen in 33 games)

## Vigilante
- Dying with unused shots is the usual failure: set a trigger by Day 2-3 and fire a Basic shot on a lead rather than waiting for proof. (seen in 17 games)

## Smuggler
- A swap moves every action aimed at both players: aim the Katana at a decoy swapped with the Medic's ward, or self-swap to dodge a check or shot. Both are told, so plan it in night chat. (seen in 13 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once about three are doused, or two if you're being questioned; long dousing usually ends with you hanged and nothing burned. (seen in 14 games)
