# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-11 10:01 by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 262 active entries of tips_notebook.json (50 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of every nomination and trial: each player's stated vote, the announced totals and your own. Vote as you said you would and explain any switch first; a mismatch with the totals is a lead, never a hang on its own. (seen in 30 games)
- Totals can be manipulated. A trial total above the voter count means a Ballot Stuffer, and a Fixer silently flips one player's trial votes. Yes/No nomination counts can't be altered, so they are the cleanest liar check. (seen in 2 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action. But a corroborated Lookout or Tracker naming X as the only unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof; don't let 'a visit isn't an attack' acquit X. (seen in 32 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name one concrete mechanical fit: an unexplained Evil read on a Vanilla claim, a broken visit claim, or a group result where elimination leaves one name. A role-block fully explains a missing result, so that is never a contradiction. (seen in 36 games)
- Each night write your planned target in your will, and post results publicly by day. The result of the night you die never reaches your will. (seen in 29 games)
- Answer a direct question about your night action in one plain sentence the first time it is asked; days of evasion become a case of their own. (seen in 9 games)
- Tokens die with you. Spend one-shot tokens on the first Day or night they can change a vote or remove a threat, and spend a puppet or doubled ballot at least a night before a likely death. (seen in 4 games)

## Village
- Post your role, target and exact result in your first speech after getting it, and counterclaim any copier at once with your full log. Results held back look retrofitted. (seen in 18 games)
- Never announce tonight's target, in public or by PM. A Wiretap may read every PM, and a named target tells blockers and the Katana where to go. (seen in 24 games)
- On Day 2, power roles with no result should not hint at their role or lead loudly. The most organized voice is the usual Night 2 kill. (seen in 24 games)
- Once someone is shown Yakuza mechanically, look at who defended them or pushed to delay their hang. Treat PM links as leads only, since Jesters and Executioners leave trails on purpose. (seen in 30 games)
- Before any hang, ask whether the nominee wins by being hanged. If you must hang a likely Jester, use a bare Guilty majority and keep confirmed power roles on Innocent. (seen in 16 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers deliberately. Read every cause of death, list unwatched Vanilla or unclaimed players and survivors with no protector, and hang the best suspect before the final two, when you can no longer hang them. (seen in 2 games)
- Surviving an attack proves nothing, since Arsonists and Serial Killers have Standard Defense. Ask the survivor at once who protected them. (seen in 17 games)

## Yakuza
- Don't defend a doomed teammate. If your team's votes can't stop the hang, vote Guilty with the town, because hidden Innocent votes show in the totals. (seen in 38 games)
- Send no daytime PMs about team plans, and none to teammates on Day 2; plan in night chat. (seen in 34 games)
- Fake-claim a Village role whose tokens and real visits match yours. Never claim or name a Yakuza-only role, and never switch claims once cornered. (seen in 19 games)
- Every member must submit the agreed Katana holder and target every night; one missing vote can cancel the kill. (seen in 20 games)
- Never flatly deny a visit that an FT count, map group or Lookout could expose. Agree a cover in night chat, and move the Katana off any holder a watcher has named. (seen in 22 games)
- After a failed Katana attack, kill or block the likely protector instead of retrying blindly. Prioritize running counts (Fortune Teller, Census Taker) and Party Animals with block logs. (seen in 21 games)
- Use Clerk rigs, puppets and cleans early, but never rig a Day when a teammate is the likely nominee. (seen in 20 games)

## Neutral
- If innate Defense saves you, deny being attacked. If you must explain it, pick one claim at once and never switch. (seen in 6 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil, so say so whenever you post a Not Evil result. A lone last Yakuza reads Not Evil every night. (seen in 19 games)

## Coroner
- Examine bodies that settle live questions: one side of a duplicate claim, or a hanged suspect. Post the night, target and role in one message the next Day, and skip cleaned bodies. (seen in 3 games)

## Medic
- Use free Standard protection on likely Katana targets, vary your pattern, and never announce targets. Spend a token on self-protection within a night or two of claiming, and never claim you 'saw' a save. (seen in 24 games)

## Vigilante
- Set a trigger by Day 2-3 and fire a Basic shot on a real lead rather than dying with all tokens. A Basic shot that fails without a credible Medic points to innate Defense, so post it. (seen in 11 games)

## Smuggler
- A swap moves only actions aimed at the two swapped players. Aim the Katana at a decoy and swap the decoy with the protected real target, or swap yourself with a Villager to dodge a check. Plan it in night chat rather than vetoing it. (seen in 2 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once 3 living players are doused, or 2 if you are being questioned. Have a claim ready that explains your douse visits. (seen in 12 games)
