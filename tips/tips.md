# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-18 08:30 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 424 active entries of tips_notebook.json (103 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules 7c0f9435c3bc058c.

## Most often decisive
- Only the Mayor, Magistrate, Vulture, Hitman and Saint are Unique, so a second claimant of any other role is often real; tables have repeatedly hanged a real holder as the 'later copier' on timing alone. A duplicate is evidence for neither side, and both can be fake, so test both that Day or night: give each a target whose visits are known, check their visits against the counts, and aim an Oracle guess or Chronicler mark at one. (seen in 53 games)
- Before you vote Guilty, name one corroborated mechanical fit, checked against the rules text: a Yakuza-only role claim, a first-person slip, a knowledge leak, a corroborated lone visitor, or a group result whose other members are confirmed. One unverified result, a wording quibble or a stack of leans usually isn't enough. Once one more night kill could bring the lock, hang your best case. Remorseful players: a Guilty that hangs a Villager kills you too, so claim the trait before the vote and vote Innocent on thin cases. (seen in 83 games)
- Yakuza: defending a doomed teammate usually backfires, because the town compares totals with stated votes and remembers defenders. Do the vote math in night chat; save the teammate only if your votes really stop the hang, otherwise vote Guilty and say so. Always state the vote you actually cast. (seen in 68 games)
- Yakuza: keep team plans out of daytime PMs, which a Wiretap or Eavesdropper may read, and send none on a Day a teammate is under pressure. Everyone sees that a PM was sent, and a visible PM triangle tends to become the town's lead for the rest of the game. (seen in 77 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows a player is Yakuza, look next at who defended them or PM'd them; a No on the nomination then a Guilty once the hang is certain is a strong tell. A caught liar or lone Evil read isn't shown Yakuza (Jesters and Notorious players read Evil too), so chaining from them often lands on Villagers. (seen in 57 games)

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret: keep a notepad of each player's stated votes and the announced totals. When a total doesn't fit, ask each voter once what they cast and how it counted. Miscasts, Merciful or Zealot traits and lying Neutrals or Turncoats explain most gaps, so don't hunt a phantom Fixer or hang on a mismatch alone. More votes than voters (after a revealed Mayor's double) means a Ballot Stuffer; with a Fixer shown, one extra Guilty on a Village claimant's trial means the Yakuza wanted that hang. (seen in 25 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens the first night they can swing something; they die with you. Kill tokens are the exception: they need a lead, a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits. Never fire on Day 1, at a bare claim clash, or to 'test' a claimed Medic. Basic and Standard shots can't get through a Serial Killer's or Arsonist's Standard Defense. (seen in 36 games)
- An unexplained survival proves little: innate Defense, the hidden Narrow Escape trait (once), a Shaky Katana holder whose attacks never kill, or a Paranoid fake notice can explain it. Check it against visit data and protectors' notices. Yakuza: if one member's attacks fail on different targets, move the Katana to a teammate told they have Katana Expert or Master. (seen in 28 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not what the visitor did. A lone visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof only when corroborated by a second source or the named players; one unverified watch is just its claimant's word. (seen in 52 games)

## Village
- Once a result bears on a vote, post the night, target and exact result in your next speech; one held back until someone claims looks retrofitted. While a Ritualist may be alive, name your exact role only when it changes a vote, and put your log in your will first. (seen in 48 games)
- On Days 1-2, don't hint at power roles or steer the table; leader-like voices are the usual Night 1 kill. Never fake a Vanilla claim: a later switch to a power role reads as cornered. (seen in 45 games)
- Don't announce tonight's target or that you'll act tonight, in public or by PM: a Wiretap may read every PM, and announcements tell blockers and the Katana where to go. (seen in 53 games)
- Read every cause of death: a Serial Killer, Arsonist or Werewolf cause proves that killer is alive. From Day 2 keep a written Neutral-killer suspect list: quiet claimants nobody has watched, survivors no protector claims, players behind unexplained visits in the counts. A Not Evil read doesn't clear them. While two Yakuza may be alive near the lock, give the hang to your Yakuza lead: hanging a Neutral leaves the Yakuza count unchanged. (seen in 23 games)
- Count the lock in effective votes and post it each morning: Yakuza plus fixed Innocents (the nominee, a declared Merciful player, a likely Turncoat) against everyone else. The Day before the lock is your last real hang, so try your strongest case and refuse 'one more night'. A Turncoat (a Village role that wins only with the Yakuza) reads Evil to alignment checks but Good in a census; spot one protecting, saving and PMing the main suspect. (seen in 18 games)
- Run a Jester check before every hang: an Evil read on someone who self-nominated, asked for the rope or claims nothing fits a Jester. If you hang one anyway, agree a named bare-majority Guilty list and keep power roles on Innocent, since every Guilty voter is a ticket in the revenge draw. (seen in 30 games)

## Yakuza
- Agree each member's cover in Night 1 chat: a real Village role that fits your tokens and real visits. Never claim a Yakuza-only role, or Vanilla unless the setup lists it. Claim your real visits as your targets and check claims against posted visit results. Once claimed, keep it; switching after a break usually convicts you. (seen in 35 games)
- Open every night-chat message with 'Holder: X. Target: Y. My action: Z.' Messages are cut at 200 words, and with two Yakuza one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 45 games)
- Rank kills by future output: Census Takers, then passive count and group roles (Scout, Cartographer, Fortune Teller), then exact-role checkers; an investigator whose results are public can wait. Expect mirrors, charms and traps on claimed shooters. (seen in 43 games)
- After a failed attack, assume a protector or Defense and remove the protector before retrying, but a checker who survived still has results coming. (seen in 42 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, so a clean read on a suspected attacker clears nothing, and with one Yakuza left late checks are near worthless. Post an Evil read as 'fits a Yakuza, Jester, Notorious or framed player', not 'Yakuza'. (seen in 29 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (one side of a duplicate claim, a hanged suspect, a disputed claimant) on the first night it exists, and post night, target and exact role in one message. Skip undisputed Katana victims and cleaned bodies, and never announce tomorrow's exam. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard the likeliest Katana targets and self-protect with a token the night after you claim. Spend Strong tokens once a Serial Killer or Arsonist death appears. Avoid possible Veterans and predictable patterns. (seen in 43 games)

## Vigilante
- Dying with unused tokens is the usual failure: set a trigger by Day 2-3 and fire Basic on a real lead. When a Neutral killer is the last enemy, fire Strong or hold: weaker shots only kill Villagers. (seen in 31 games)
