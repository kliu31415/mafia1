# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-13 00:25 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 349 active entries of tips_notebook.json (76 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of every stated vote and announced total; a mismatch is a lead to ask about once, not proof. (seen in 17 games)
- When totals don't fit stated votes, the usual cause is a miscast or misstated ballot, so re-check reminders before chasing a Fixer or Ballot Stuffer. If your own vote seems to count opposite, say so and switch it next trial. (seen in 8 games)
- Dead players can't spend tokens, so it usually pays to use one-shot tokens the first time they can swing a vote or remove a threat. Kill shots need a real lead; a claim clash isn't one, since duplicate claimants are often real. (seen in 19 games)
- Keep votes matching your stated reads and explain any switch first; unexplained mismatches get cited against you, though in others they are only a weak lead. (seen in 38 games)
- Keep past results and tonight's plan in your will. The night you die never reaches it, so post important results while alive. (seen in 42 games)
- Check the rules before calling a contradiction: only Smuggler and Vulture are unique, and a role-block fully explains a missing result. (seen in 42 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action; a lone visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof only with independent corroboration. (seen in 40 games)

## Village
- Hunt Neutral killers on purpose: a non-Katana cause of death or a multi-death night proves one, so list unclaimed players, late Vanilla claims and unexplained attack survivors; a Not Evil read clears none. Count the vote lock first: with two Yakuza possibly alive at 5-6 players, the hang usually belongs on the best Yakuza lead. (seen in 12 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name a concrete contradiction or mechanical fit, not a lean. But when no result can come, 'wait for results' is a stall: hang the best lead before the Yakuza hold half the votes. (seen in 59 games)
- Investigators: post target and exact result the first Day it bears on a vote; results revealed late tend to look retrofitted. (seen in 35 games)
- It rarely pays to hint at a power role on Days 1-2 or announce tonight's target, even by PM; that usually draws the block or the Katana. (seen in 43 games)
- A public claim of a Yakuza-only role is close to a confession: hang it that Day. Once someone is mechanically shown Yakuza, their defenders and daily PM partners are the next leads. (seen in 43 games)
- A player who self-nominates or reads Evil while inviting the rope is often a Jester. Prefer another hang, or keep Guilty votes to a bare majority: revenge hits a random Guilty voter. (seen in 21 games)

## Yakuza
- Defending a doomed teammate usually costs more than it saves: unless the team's votes can stop the hang, vote Guilty and say so. Village: more Innocent votes than players admitting them marks a hidden defender. (seen in 53 games)
- Plan only in night chat. Everyone sees who PMs whom, and a pair who PM daily and vote alike is usually the town's best lead. (seen in 56 games)
- Confirm holder and target every night; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 38 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1 that fit your tokens and real visits. Audits and exact-role checks break mismatched claims, and switching when cornered is usually fatal. (seen in 29 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow your team (Census Taker, Oracle, Accountant, visit counters); near parity, a Vigilante or Sniper with shots comes first. (seen in 28 games)
- After a failed Katana, assume a protector or innate Defense and remove the protector rather than retry; move the Katana off any member a watcher has placed at a victim. (seen in 31 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, and Jesters, Executioners and Ex-Cons read Evil, so post reads with those caveats. (seen in 23 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (a duplicate claimant, a hanged suspect) the first night it exists and post the role next Day; announcing your target usually gets you killed. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard likely Katana targets with free protection, unannounced. Standard doesn't stop a Serial Killer or ignite, so spend a token on Strong once such a killer is shown. (seen in 33 games)

## Vigilante
- Dying with unused shots is the usual failure: fire a Basic shot on a lead by Day 2-3. Only a Strong shot beats a Neutral killer's Defense. (seen in 17 games)

## Clerk
- Rig a Day when a non-teammate can be nominated but not convicted, not one where a teammate is the likely nominee. Village: with a rig possible, every Yes is a vote to hang. (seen in 19 games)

## Smuggler
- A swap moves every action aimed at either swapped player: aim the Katana at a decoy and swap it with the Medic's likely ward, or self-swap with a Villager to dodge a check or shot. Both are told, so prepare a cover. (seen in 13 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once three players are doused, or two if you're being questioned; each extra dousing night lets the town hang you first. (seen in 14 games)

## Jester
- Build a believable Village-side case with one shaky claim; repeated self-nominations usually make the town refuse to hang you. (seen in 9 games)
