# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-17 14:03 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 419 active entries of tips_notebook.json (101 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules a4bd03b31963e907.

## Most often decisive
- Only the Mayor, Vulture, Hitman and Saint are Unique, so two claimants of one role can both be real. Two real holders often pick the same targets, and towns have repeatedly hanged the real one as the later 'copier'. But 'both can be real' isn't evidence for either: test both that Day or night with an exact-role check, a watch or the visit counts. (seen in 52 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name a corroborated mechanical break: a claim of a Yakuza-only role, a first-person slip, a knowledge leak, or a claimed visit that corroborated logs contradict. One unverified player's track, map or count is only a claim, and an impossible result may be a Roleplayer's random noise, not grounds to hang. Near the vote lock, hang your best case. Remorseful players: vote Guilty only on such a break and declare the trait first. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village, so look again at who pushed it. (seen in 81 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows a player is Yakuza, those who defended them, pushed to delay the hang or traded PMs with them are usually the next leads. A caught lie or a single Evil read doesn't show that: Jesters, other Chaos Neutrals, framed and Notorious players all read Evil, so run the Jester check first. (seen in 55 games)
- Yakuza: send teammates no daytime PMs, and none at all on a Day a teammate is under pressure. Everyone sees that a PM was sent, and a Wiretap may read it. Teams that sent none were caught only through mechanics. (seen in 75 games)
- Yakuza: defend a doomed teammate only if your votes can save them. Otherwise vote Yes and Guilty and say so. The town compares totals with stated votes, so a hidden Innocent tends to expose the next teammate. (seen in 68 games)

## Everyone
- Keep a notepad of every nomination and trial: each player's stated vote, the totals and your own ballot. A stated vote that doesn't fit a small total is usually a miscast: ask once, and never hang on it. If a gap remains, ask each voter what they cast and how it counted. Rule out Merciful, Zealot, and lying Neutrals or a Turncoat before suspecting a Fixer. A leftover extra Guilty in a Village claimant's trial suggests a Fixer bought the hang. No role changes Yes/No counts, so they're the cleanest check for a lie. (seen in 24 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens the first time they can change a vote: they die with you. Kill tokens need a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits. Never fire over a claim clash, to 'test' a claimed Medic, or at a Neutral that never blocks wins. At parity, fire even on a coin flip. (seen in 34 games)
- A visit shows where someone went, not what they did. A lone visitor to a Katana victim is near proof only if the report is corroborated: one unverified watcher's report is easy for a Jester or a Yakuza to invent. (seen in 52 games)
- Keep your will current: past results, tonight's plan and what each outcome would mean. The result of the night you die never reaches your will, so post key results while you're alive. (seen in 56 games)
- Surviving a Katana attack with no protector named fits innate Defense (Serial Killer, Arsonist, Jester). It also fits a Shaky holder's level-None attacks, a one-time Narrow Escape, a Stolen Blade or a fake Paranoid notice. Check visit counts and other failed attacks before hanging the survivor. Yakuza: move the Katana off a member whose attacks keep failing. (seen in 28 games)
- A game has at most one Turncoat: a Villager who wins only with the Yakuza, reads Evil to alignment checks and counts as Good in a census. A claimant who keeps protecting the main suspect and voting to save them may be the Turncoat: count them as a Yakuza vote. If you are the Turncoat, work out the Yakuza from public results and offer help in a short PM. (seen in 2 games)

## Village
- Post a result that bears on a vote in your first speech after getting it, with the night, target and exact wording. Results held back look made up. While a Ritualist may be in play, claim only as much of your role as the result needs, and put your full log in your will first. (seen in 47 games)
- Don't hint at your role on Day 1 or 2: the loudest organizer or hinter is usually the Night 1 Katana target. Never announce tonight's target, or that you will act, in public or by PM. (seen in 53 games)
- Read every cause of death: a Serial Killer, Werewolf or ignite death proves that killer is alive. Keep a Neutral-killer suspect list (unclaimed or unwatched players, unexplained survivors, gaps in the visit counts) and hang them while the table is large. Count the vote lock in effective votes: the Yakuza plus every sure Innocent (the nominee, a declared Merciful, a likely Turncoat). Near the lock, hang your best Yakuza lead, never a Neutral that never blocks wins while the Katana still kills. The Day before the lock is usually your last real hang, so refuse 'one more night'. (seen in 22 games)
- Signs of a Jester: self-nominations, asking for the rope, an Evil read with no role claim. Prefer to leave them alive. If you hang one, agree on a named bare majority plus a buffer vote, and have confirmed power roles and Cursed or Bonded players vote Innocent. (seen in 29 games)

## Yakuza
- Agree your cover claims in Night 1 chat: real Village roles that fit your tokens, your visits and every posted visit result. Avoid Vanilla, Yakuza-only roles and roles a living player has claimed. Switching claims after yours breaks tends to seal the hang. (seen in 35 games)
- Every member votes for the agreed holder and target every night. Start with 'Holder: X. Target: Y.', since night-chat messages are cut after 200 words. (seen in 44 games)
- Choose kills by what the victim could still find: Census Takers first, then roles that count visits or groups, exact-role checkers and working blockers, all ahead of investigators whose results are already public. (seen in 41 games)
- Protectors stack on the Day 1 organizer and fresh claimants, and mirrors have killed Katana holders on Night 1. Hit a quieter, capable player, with your most expendable member holding the Katana. After a failed attack, remove the protector rather than retrying. (seen in 42 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil, so a clean read clears little, though unchecked players still rank above clean ones. Once only one Yakuza is left, Not Evil clears no one. (seen in 29 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (one side of a duplicate claim, a hanged suspect) on the first night it exists. Skip cleaned bodies and undisputed Katana victims. Post night, target and role in one message. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Protect vocal analysts early, then claimed investigators, and never visit a possible Veteran. Free protection doesn't stop Strong attacks (Serial Killer, ignite), so spend tokens on Strong protection once a Neutral killer shows, and on protecting yourself the night after you claim. (seen in 43 games)

## Vigilante
- Fire a Basic shot on a real lead by Day 2-3 rather than die holding tokens. If it fails on a suspect nobody protects, they probably have Defense or Narrow Escape: shoot again with 2 tokens. (seen in 30 games)
