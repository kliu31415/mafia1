# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-10 22:03 by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 247 active entries of tips_notebook.json (46 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad: for every nomination and trial, log each player's stated vote, the announced totals and your own vote, and flag any stated vote that changes or that the totals contradict (e.g. more Guilty votes than players who said Guilty). Make your own ballot match your stated read, and explain any change before the vote. (seen in 30 games)
- You can't use tokens once you're dead. Spend one-shot tokens (rig, puppet, ritual guess, shots, swaps) on the first Day or night they can change a vote or remove a threat. A puppet or doubled ballot lapses if you die that night, so spend it at least a night before a likely death. (seen in 18 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action: a watch can't tell protection from a frame or an attack. But if a corroborated Lookout shows X as the only unexplained visitor to a Katana victim, treat it as near-proof, and don't let 'a visit isn't an attack' acquit X. (seen in 29 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name one concrete mechanical contradiction, such as a broken claim, an Evil result against a Vanilla claim, a group elimination or a lone visitor. A role-block fully explains a missing result, roles can repeat unless proven unique, and a contradiction alone doesn't separate a Yakuza from a Jester who wants the hang. (seen in 32 games)
- Before each night, write your planned target in your will. Results from the night you die never reach your will, so post every result publicly by day. (seen in 27 games)
- Answer a question about your night action in one plain sentence the first time it is asked. A dodge, or a denial you later reverse, becomes the case against you. (seen in 15 games)

## Village
- Post your role, night, target and exact result in your first speech after getting it, and counterclaim at once with your full log if someone copies you. A held-back result looks retrofitted. (seen in 16 games)
- Don't hint at a power role on Day 1. Never announce tonight's target, publicly or by PM: it tells the Pharmacist whom to block and the Katana where to strike. (seen in 24 games)
- Once a player is mechanically shown Yakuza, suspect those who defended them or argued to delay. Ask for stated votes before each vote and compare them with the totals; a No on the nomination followed by Guilty once the hang is certain is the telling pattern. (seen in 27 games)
- A PM link or a pile of soft leans is a lead, never a hang: Jesters and Executioners leave PM trails on purpose. Require a mechanical result before convicting. (seen in 28 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers from Day 2. Read every cause of death: an ignite or Serial Killer attack proves that killer is alive. Keep a written suspect list (unwatched Vanilla claimants, players who survived attacks with no credible protector, unexplained visit gaps) and spend a hang on the best suspect before the endgame, because at two alive you can't hang them. (seen in 17 games)
- Before any hang, ask whether the nominee wins by being hanged: watch for self-nominations, eager-to-be-tried claims and easily disproved fake results. If you must hang a possible Jester, keep Guilty votes to a bare majority and have confirmed power roles vote Innocent. (seen in 15 games)
- Surviving an attack confirms nothing, since Arsonists and Serial Killers have Standard Defense and Jesters and Executioners have Basic. Ask the survivor what explains their Defense, and test the answer with an audit or a watch. (seen in 15 games)

## Yakuza
- Don't defend a doomed teammate unless the team's votes can actually stop the hang; otherwise vote Guilty and say so. On a likely Jester, keep some of you on Innocent, because every Guilty voter is in the revenge draw. (seen in 34 games)
- Never PM teammates by day: an Eavesdropper or Wiretap reads it, and a visible PM triangle becomes the town's lead. Plan in night chat. (seen in 30 games)
- Agree a fake claim in night chat before Day 2 that fits your token count and real visits. Never claim a Yakuza-only role or a unique role, and never switch claims once cornered. (seen in 18 games)
- Every member submits the agreed Katana holder and target every night; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 19 games)
- Give the Katana to a low-value member, and move it the same night once a Lookout or Tracker names the holder. After a failed attack, remove the protector instead of retrying. (seen in 21 games)

## Neutral
- If you survive an attack, nobody else knows you were targeted, so don't announce it. If you must explain it, pick one claim at once and keep it. (seen in 6 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, so a Not Evil result on a suspected attacker clears nothing; say so when you post it. Once only one Yakuza can be alive, no Not Evil result clears anyone. (seen in 16 games)

## Medic
- Your free Standard protection stops the Katana: use it on vocal analysts and claimed investigators, and rotate rather than follow a predictable pattern. Self-protect with a token the night you claim, and never say you 'confirmed' a save, since you get no feedback. (seen in 21 games)

## Vigilante
- Shoot on a lead, not proof: a claim conflict, an Evil result against an unverifiable claim, or a defender of an exposed Yakuza. Dying with all your tokens is the usual failure. A failed Basic shot hints at innate Defense, so post it. (seen in 11 games)

## Clerk
- Rig a Day when a townie can win a nomination but not a Guilty majority, never when a teammate is the likely nominee. On the rigged trial, teammates vote Innocent, because a small Guilty total exposes them. (seen in 18 games)

## Smuggler
- A swap moves exactly the actions aimed at the two swapped players, so don't veto it out of fear. To beat a Medic, aim the Katana at a decoy and swap the decoy with your real target. To dodge a check, swap yourself with a Villager. Never swap the target unless you mean to move the kill, and have a cover ready for both swap visits. (seen in 7 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once 3 or more players are doused, or 2 if you're being questioned. Douse players likely to survive, not today's hang targets. (seen in 12 games)
