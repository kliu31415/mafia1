# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-13 12:45 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 375 active entries of tips_notebook.json (82 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules 7f195f57f7251c87.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret: keep a notepad of stated votes and announced totals, and vote as you said you would. A mismatch is usually a lead, not a hang. (seen in 38 games)
- When totals don't fit stated votes, a miscast or misstated ballot is likeliest: ask voters to re-check reminders before hunting a Fixer. More votes than voters does show a Ballot Stuffer. (seen in 9 games)
- Tokens die with you: spend one-shot tokens the first time they can swing a vote or stop a threat. Kill shots usually need a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits, not a claim clash or 'test'. (seen in 23 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action, and players can visit twice; still, a corroborated lone unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is close to proof. (seen in 43 games)
- Only Smuggler and Vulture are Unique, so duplicate claimants can both be real: test both rather than hanging or shooting on the clash. (seen in 43 games)
- Keep past results, tonight's target and how to read each outcome in your will; the result of the night you die never reaches it. (seen in 48 games)
- One unexplained survival proves little: hidden Narrow Escape saves anyone once, and a Shaky Yakuza's Katana never kills. A second failed attack with no protector points to real Defense.

## Village
- Before voting Guilty, name a mechanical break: a Yakuza-only role claim, a broken claim, a corroborated visit, a slip about an attack. Echoed leans and wording quibbles tend to hang Villagers. (seen in 64 games)
- Count the vote lock each morning: once Yakuza hold half the living votes no nomination passes, so 'wait for tonight's results' usually just costs an investigator. Hang the best lead first. (seen in 18 games)
- Post night, target and exact result once it bears on a vote, but hinting at your role on Day 1 or announcing tonight's target usually draws the block, the Katana or a Ritualist's guess. (seen in 46 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows a Yakuza, look at who argued delay beforehand, defended them or PM'd them; teammates often vote Guilty, so totals alone hide them. (seen in 47 games)
- A pair who PM every Day and push together is a lead: ask each publicly what was said, and convict on the pair plus one independent break. (seen in 42 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers on purpose: read every cause of death and press quiet uncheckable claimants; Not Evil doesn't clear a Serial Killer or Arsonist. Near the lock the hang usually belongs on the Yakuza lead. (seen in 13 games)
- A player who self-nominates or pushes without data is often a Jester: usually leave them alive, or hang with a bare Guilty majority, since revenge hits a Guilty voter. (seen in 25 games)
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, and a lone last Yakuza always holds it, so late Not Evil reads clear nobody. (seen in 25 games)
- A Turncoat (a Villager who wins with the Yakuza) reads Evil but counts Good in a census; hanging one doesn't lower the Yakuza count.
- If Remorseful, vote Guilty only on corroborated cases: a wrong Guilty on a Villager kills you. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village, so re-examine who pushed it.

## Yakuza
- Daytime PMs between teammates tend to become the town's main lead; plan in night chat only. (seen in 60 games)
- Unless the vote math can save them, vote Guilty on a doomed teammate and say so: defenders and stray Innocents are whom the town hunts next. (seen in 56 games)
- Confirm holder and target in night chat and submit every night; with two alive, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 41 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1: a real Village role fitting your tokens and visits, not Vanilla or a Yakuza-only role. Switching claims when cornered usually hangs you. (seen in 30 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow your team (Census Taker, visit counters, exact-role checkers); near parity a Vigilante or Sniper comes first. (seen in 32 games)
- After a failed Katana, assume protection and block or kill the likely protector rather than retrying blindly. (seen in 32 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim) on the first night it exists and post it next Day, without announcing targets. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard likely Katana targets (vocal analysts, claimed investigators) without announcing it. Free protection doesn't stop Strong attacks, so spend tokens once a Serial Killer or Arsonist shows. (seen in 34 games)

## Vigilante
- Dying with unused shots is the usual failure: set a trigger by Day 2-3 and fire a Basic shot on a real lead before the Yakuza near parity. (seen in 20 games)

## Smuggler
- Use swaps rather than hoarding them: aim the Katana at a decoy swapped with the Medic's likely ward (kill and guard trade places), or swap a suspected teammate with a Villager so checks and shots land elsewhere. Both swapped players are told. (seen in 14 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite at about three living douses, or two once questioned; each extra dousing night risks a hang or a wasted douse. (seen in 15 games)
