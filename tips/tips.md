# Tips from past games

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of every stated vote and announced total. A stated vote the totals contradict is a lead to question, rarely enough to hang on. (seen in 19 games)
- When totals don't fit stated votes, a miscast or misstated ballot is the usual cause: have voters re-check reminders before hunting a Fixer or Ballot Stuffer. If your own vote seems reversed, say so and switch your ballot that Day. (seen in 9 games)
- Tokens die with you: spend one-shot tokens the first time they can swing a vote or remove a threat. Village kill tokens are the exception: fire on a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits, not a claim clash or coin flip. (seen in 25 games)
- Before each night, put past results, tonight's target and how to read each outcome in your will; the result of the night you die never reaches it. (seen in 50 games)
- One unexplained Katana survival proves little: besides protection or innate Defense, a hidden Shaky holder's Katana never kills and hidden Narrow Escape saves anyone once. Yakuza: if one member's attacks keep failing, move the Katana. (seen in 25 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action. A corroborated lone unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof; an uncorroborated report is only that claimant's word. (seen in 43 games)

## Village
- Read every cause of death and keep a Neutral-killer suspect list (quiet unclaimed players, unexplained survivors): an unhunted Serial Killer or Arsonist often wins. Near the vote lock, though, the hang usually belongs on your best Yakuza lead. (seen in 14 games)
- Count the vote lock each morning: once the Yakuza hold half the living players, no nomination passes. Hang your best case the Day before rather than waiting for results. (seen in 12 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name a mechanical break: a broken or Yakuza-only role claim, a corroborated lone visit, a token count the claim can't explain. Leans and wording are weak, but 'wait for results' when none is coming is a stall. (seen in 66 games)
- Avoid hinting at a power role or announcing tonight's target, even by PM; it tends to draw the block or the Katana. Once a result bears on a vote, post night, target and exact result. (seen in 47 games)
- When a mechanical result shows a Yakuza, look next at who defended them, urged delay or PM'd them. A caught liar or lone Evil read may be a Jester instead. (seen in 48 games)
- A player who self-nominates or pushes hangs without evidence is often a Jester: usually leave them alive, and if you hang, keep Guilty votes to a bare majority because revenge hits a Guilty voter. (seen in 25 games)
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, and Serial Killers and Arsonists always do, so a clean Sheriff read clears little, least of all with one Yakuza left. (seen in 25 games)
- An Evil read plus a Good census count fits a Turncoat or Notorious Villager, and hanging a Turncoat doesn't lower the Yakuza count. Turncoats can't see the Yakuza, so they usually vote with the town.
- If you are Remorseful, vote Guilty only on corroborated cases, since hanging a Villager kills you too. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village: re-examine whoever built that case.

## Yakuza
- It rarely pays to defend a doomed teammate: announced totals expose a hidden Innocent. Unless your votes can actually stop the hang, vote Guilty and say so. (seen in 57 games)
- Keep team talk in night chat. Daytime PMs between teammates are visible to all, can be read, and tend to become the town's pair lead. (seen in 62 games)
- Confirm Katana holder and target in night chat every night; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 41 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1: a Village role that fits your tokens and real visits. Claiming a Yakuza-only role or switching claims when cornered is usually fatal. (seen in 31 games)
- Kill the investigator whose next result would narrow your team; after a failed attack, remove the likely protector rather than retry blindly. (seen in 33 games)
- With a Smuggler, aim the Katana at a decoy and swap it with the guarded target: the kill lands there and the save moves away. Self-swapping a suspected member sends checks onto a Villager; swapped players are told. (seen in 5 games)

## Neutral
- Arsonist: ignite once three are doused, or two if you're being questioned; each extra dousing night risks your hang and loses douses to other deaths. (seen in 15 games)
- Solo killers: pick one tokenless cover early and keep it; switching claims or admitting an unexplained survival points at innate Defense. (seen in 7 games)

## Coroner
- Examine a body that settles a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim) the first night you can, and post night, target and role next Day. Announcing tomorrow's exam tends to make you tonight's kill. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard the likeliest Katana target (claimed investigators, hang leaders) unannounced. Free protection is only Standard, so spend a token for Strong once a Serial Killer or Arsonist shows. (seen in 34 games)
