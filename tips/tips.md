# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-17 01:21 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 407 active entries of tips_notebook.json (99 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules 8f2138dae76eb0b8.

## Most often decisive
- Only the Mayor, Vulture, Hitman and Saint are Unique. Any other role can have two real claimants, and two real holders often pick the same targets and get the same results. Don't hang the later claimant on timing or the clash alone. Both claims can also be fake, so test both that Day or night: ask each to target a player whose visits are known, aim a watch or an exact-role check at one, and look for a detail that couldn't have been copied. (seen in 50 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name one corroborated break: a claim of a Yakuza-only role, a first-person slip ('my Katana'), a leak of night knowledge, or a group result whose other members are confirmed. One unverified claimant's map, count or watch is only their word. It may be Roleplayer noise or a lie, and a visit shows where someone went, not what they did there. Discount such a result rather than hang its target. When the vote lock is one night away, hang your best case anyway. (seen in 79 games)
- Yakuza: don't PM teammates during the Day, and send no PMs at all on a Day a teammate is under pressure. Everyone sees that a PM was sent, and a Wiretap or Eavesdropper may read it. Visible PM triangles tend to become the town's lead for the rest of the game. (seen in 73 games)
- Yakuza: don't defend a doomed teammate. The totals are compared with what everyone said, so a hidden Innocent gets pinned on whoever defended. Vote Guilty and say so, unless the team's votes can actually save them. (seen in 67 games)
- Village: once a mechanical result shows a player is Yakuza, your next suspects are usually the players who argued to delay the hang, defended them or PM'd them. A caught liar or a lone Evil Sheriff read doesn't show Yakuza, because Jesters and other Chaos Neutrals read Evil too. (seen in 55 games)

## Everyone
- Keep a notepad. For every nomination and trial, write each player's stated vote and the announced totals. A stated vote that doesn't fit a small total is usually a miscast. Ask that player once to re-check their reminder and post what they cast and how it counted, and treat it as a lead, not a hang. Other plain causes are a lie (often from a Neutral or a Turncoat) or a Merciful or Zealot trait. Assume a Fixer only once something shows one exists. Then, if a Village claimant's trial has one more Guilty than was stated, the Yakuza probably wanted that hang. (seen in 23 games)
- An unexplained survival has several possible causes: innate Defense (Serial Killer or Arsonist Standard, Jester or Executioner Basic), the hidden Narrow Escape trait (works once), a hidden Shaky holder whose Katana never kills, or a fake notice from the Paranoid trait. Ask the survivor once for the exact notice and check it against the visit data. Failed attacks on different targets fit one Shaky holder, so the Yakuza should then move the Katana. (seen in 28 games)
- A Turncoat is a Villager who wins only with the Yakuza. They read Evil to alignment checks but count as Good in a census. Spot one by behaviour: they keep protecting or voting to save the main suspect. Count them as a Yakuza vote in the lock math. (seen in 2 games)
- Answer direct questions about your night action in one plain sentence the first time. Evasion repeated over several Days becomes a case on its own. (seen in 29 games)
- Before each night, write your planned target in your will, with what each possible result would mean. Results from the night you die never reach your will, so post important results publicly while you are alive. (seen in 52 games)

## Village
- Investigators: once a result bears on a vote, post the night, the target and the exact result. A result held back until someone accuses you looks retrofitted. While a Ritualist may be alive, put the full log in your will before you claim. (seen in 46 games)
- Don't announce tonight's target or token use, in public or by PM. It tells blockers whom to block and tells the Katana where to strike. (seen in 52 games)
- On Day 1 and Day 2, don't hint at a power role or lead the discussion without a result. The loudest organizer is often the Night 1 Katana target. If you're pressed, claim plainly once rather than dodging. (seen in 42 games)
- Count down to the vote lock in effective votes: the Yakuza, plus the accused's own vote, declared Merciful voters and likely Turncoats. On the Day before the lock, hang your strongest case. Don't accept 'one more night of checks'. (seen in 15 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers deliberately. Read every cause of death and keep a suspect list: unclaimed players and claimants nobody has checked. Near the lock, though, give the hang to your best Yakuza lead, because hanging a Neutral killer doesn't lower the Yakuza count. Never spend a hang on a Neutral that never blocks wins. (seen in 21 games)
- Jester signs: a self-nomination, pushes with no data, an Evil read on someone who invites the rope. Leave them alive if you can. If you do hang one, agree a named Guilty list with a buffer vote or two, and keep confirmed power roles and Cursed or Bonded players voting Innocent. (seen in 29 games)
- If you are Remorseful, a Guilty vote on a Village player who is then hanged kills you too. Vote Guilty only on corroborated mechanical cases, and otherwise vote Innocent and say 'insurance Innocent'.

## Yakuza
- Submit the agreed Katana holder, target and night action every night. With two Yakuza alive, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 44 games)
- Agree each member's cover in Night 1 chat. Pick a real Village role that fits your tokens and actual visits, never Vanilla Villager or any Yakuza-only role. Before claiming, check the cover against every posted visit result (counts, groups, watches, Insomniac lists), and name your real visit destinations as your targets. Don't switch claims once cornered: the switch is usually a case on its own. (seen in 34 games)
- Usually kill whoever's next result could expose you, Census Takers and counting roles first. Claimed shooters are often guarded, so deal with the protector first. After a failed attack, don't retry blindly; remove or block the protector instead. (seen in 42 games)
- Never speak from your hidden perspective ('my Katana', who was blocked). A vague retraction of a slip reads as a confession. (seen in 14 games)

## Neutral
- Arsonist: ignite once three or more players are doused, or as soon as you are questioned. Losing douses to other deaths and trials is the usual way to lose. (seen in 17 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, so a clean read on a suspected attacker clears nothing. While several Yakuza may be alive, still rank unchecked players above Not Evil reads. (seen in 29 games)

## Coroner
- Examine a body that settles a live question (one side of a duplicate claim, a hanged suspect) on the first night one exists. Post the night, target and role, and never announce tomorrow's exam. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Protect vocal analysts early and claimed investigators later. Stay away from Veterans. Spend tokens on Strong protection once a Strong attacker appears, and on self-protection the night after you claim. (seen in 43 games)

## Vigilante
- Dying with unused tokens is the usual failure. Shoot on a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits by Day 2-3. Never shoot on a claim clash or to 'test' a claimed Medic. If your shot kills, post the role you learned. (seen in 29 games)
