# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-12 12:22 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 334 active entries of tips_notebook.json (71 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/.

## Everyone
- Keep a notepad of each player's stated vote on every nomination and trial, the announced totals and your own vote. Ballots are secret, so a stated vote the totals contradict is a lead to question (usually a miscast), not a hang by itself. (seen in 14 games)
- Totals can be manipulated: a Ballot Stuffer adds a trial vote and a Fixer silently flips one player's. When a total doesn't fit, first ask voters to re-check their reminders; nomination counts can't be altered, so they are the cleaner liar test. (seen in 6 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens the first time they can change a vote or remove a threat; dying with them is the common waste. Kill tokens need a lead first (not a bare claim clash: duplicate claimants are often both real), but fire by parity. (seen in 15 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action. A corroborated lone unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is still near-proof; an uncorroborated report is only the claimant's word. (seen in 39 games)
- Each Day, put past results and tonight's plan in your will. The result of the night you die never reaches it, so post what matters publicly. (seen in 39 games)
- Keep votes matching your stated reads and answer direct questions about your night in one plain sentence; evasion tends to become the case against you. (seen in 36 games)
- Check the rules before calling a contradiction: only Smuggler and Vulture are unique, a role-block explains a missing result, and a swap explains an odd one. (seen in 38 games)

## Village
- Hunt Neutral killers on purpose: read every cause of death (a Serial Killer or ignite cause proves one alive), and Not Evil doesn't clear them. Press unwatched Vanilla claimants and unexplained attack survivors, but don't spend hangs on roles that never block wins, and near the vote lock hang the Yakuza lead first. (seen in 10 games)
- Investigators: post target and exact result once it bears on a vote, because held-back results look retrofitted. It rarely pays to announce tonight's target or hint at a power role with no result: that draws the block, the Katana or a Ritualist guess. (seen in 40 games)
- Convict on mechanical cases (a broken claim, a Yakuza-only role claim, an attack slip, elimination from a corroborated result) rather than leans or wording. When no result is coming, the best lead available, such as a pair who PM daily and vote alike, is your case. (seen in 54 games)
- Count the vote lock each morning: once Yakuza hold half the living players, no nomination passes. 'Wait for tonight's results' is often a Yakuza line, and the Day before the lock is the last real hang. (seen in 14 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows someone is Yakuza, those who defended them, argued for delay or PM'd them are the next leads. (seen in 40 games)
- A player who self-nominates, pushes without evidence or reads Evil while asking for the rope is often a Jester. Usually leave them alive; if you hang, keep Guilty to a bare majority, since revenge kills a Guilty voter. (seen in 21 games)
- Survival confirms nothing: ask a Katana survivor to name a protector. Unexplained survival plus Not Evil fits a Serial Killer, and the Katana holder also reads Not Evil. (seen in 24 games)

## Yakuza
- Defending a doomed teammate usually costs more than it saves. Do the vote math in night chat; unless your votes can stop the hang, vote Guilty and say so. (seen in 50 games)
- Plan in night chat, not daytime PMs: everyone sees who messaged whom, and a PM pair often becomes the town's main lead. (seen in 51 games)
- Confirm holder and target every night and have everyone submit the vote; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 34 games)
- Agree Village covers before Day 2 that fit your tokens and real visits. Naming a Yakuza-only role is a confession, and switching claims once cornered is usually fatal. (seen in 27 games)
- Kill players whose future results narrow you (Census Taker, Oracle, Cartographer, blockers); near parity, a Vigilante or Sniper with shots comes first. (seen in 23 games)
- After a failed Katana, remove the protector rather than retry. If nobody claims the save, the survivor likely has innate Defense: hang a Serial Killer or Arsonist, because the Katana can't kill them. (seen in 29 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (a duplicate claimant, a hanged suspect) on the first night it exists and post the role next Day. Announcing your next exam invites the kill, and a cleaned body yields nothing. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Free Standard protection on claimed investigators stops the Katana but not Strong attacks; spend a token on Strong once a Neutral killer shows. Staying unclaimed tends to avoid blocks. (seen in 31 games)

## Smuggler
- Don't veto swaps for fear of redirecting your own kill: a swap moves exactly the actions aimed at the two swapped players. Aim the Katana at a decoy swapped with the Medic's likely ward, or swap yourself with a Villager to dodge a check, and tell teammates, since their actions move too. (seen in 3 games)
