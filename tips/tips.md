# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-13 08:23 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 355 active entries of tips_notebook.json (77 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/.

## Everyone
- Keep a notepad of stated votes and announced totals for every nomination and trial; a stated vote the totals contradict is a lead to question, rarely grounds to hang. (seen in 18 games)
- A total that doesn't fit stated votes is usually a miscast or misstated ballot: have voters re-check reminders before hunting a Fixer. More votes than voters proves a Ballot Stuffer. (seen in 9 games)
- Tokens die with you, so spend one-shot tokens the first time they matter. Kill shots instead need a real lead, not a claim clash (most roles can repeat) or a 'test' of a claim. (seen in 19 games)
- Keep past results and tonight's plan in your will; the night you die never reaches it, so post important results while alive. (seen in 43 games)
- Check the role text before calling a contradiction: only Smuggler and Vulture are unique, and a block or swap explains a missing or odd result. (seen in 42 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action; a lone visitor to a Katana victim is strong evidence only when corroborated. (seen in 40 games)
- Vote as you said and answer questions about your night in one plain sentence; mismatches and evasion usually become the case against you. (seen in 38 games)
- A hidden Shaky Yakuza's Katana never kills but still visits and notifies, so an unexplained Katana survival isn't always innate Defense; failures on several targets fit one Shaky holder.

## Village
- Investigators: post night, target and exact result once it bears on a vote; held-back results look retrofitted. With nothing decisive, staying unclaimed is usually safer. (seen in 36 games)
- Avoid announcing tonight's target or hinting at a power role, publicly or by PM: it tells killers and blockers where to go. (seen in 44 games)
- Convict on mechanical cases (a broken claim, a Yakuza-only role claim, a corroborated lone visit, an impossible token count), not wording or stacked leans; when no result can come, hang the best lead rather than stall. (seen in 59 games)
- Count the vote lock each morning: once Yakuza hold half the living players no nomination passes, and 'wait for tonight's results' is a favourite Yakuza line. (seen in 15 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers: a non-Katana attack cause or multi-death night proves one, and Sheriffs read them Not Evil. Press quiet unclaimed players and unexplained survivors, but near the lock hang the Yakuza lead first. (seen in 12 games)
- Once a mechanical result exposes a Yakuza, their defenders, delay-arguers and PM partners are usually the next leads. Leads, not verdicts. (seen in 44 games)
- A player self-nominating, pushing without data or courting the rope after an Evil read is often a Jester: prefer not to hang, or keep Guilty votes to a bare majority. (seen in 22 games)
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, so a clean read on a suspected attacker clears little. (seen in 23 games)
- A Turncoat is a Villager who wins with the Yakuza: Evil to Sheriff or Seer, Good in a census. Hanging one doesn't lower the Yakuza count, so near the lock prefer a Yakuza lead.
- If Remorseful, a Guilty vote on a hanged true Villager kills you, so vote Guilty only on corroborated cases. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village: re-examine who pushed it.

## Yakuza
- Do the vote math in night chat: if a teammate can't be saved, usually vote Guilty and say so, because totals are compared with stated votes. (seen in 53 games)
- Keep plans in night chat; daytime PMs between teammates tend to become the town's lead. (seen in 56 games)
- Confirm holder and target nightly; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 39 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1: a Village role matching your tokens and visits. Yakuza-only roles, Vanilla claims and cornered switches usually get tested and hanged. (seen in 29 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow your team (Census Taker, visit counters, exact-role checkers, blockers); at parity, a shooter with tokens comes first. (seen in 29 games)
- After a failed Katana assume a protector or innate Defense: remove the protector or push the survivor's hang, and move the Katana if one member keeps failing. (seen in 31 games)
- Move the Katana off any member a watcher, map or count has placed at a victim. (seen in 26 games)

## Neutral
- Solo killers: settle early on one tokenless cover and take a stance; quiet late claimants get hanged. Arsonists usually do better igniting at two or three douses than waiting. (seen in 14 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim) on the first night possible, post it next Day, and don't announce your target. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard likely Katana targets without naming them; free Standard protection doesn't stop Strong attacks, so spend a token once a Neutral killer shows. (seen in 33 games)

## Smuggler
- A swap moves every action aimed at either player: aim the Katana at a decoy swapped with the Medic's likely ward, or self-swap to dodge a check or shot. Both players are told, so prepare a cover. (seen in 13 games)

## Jester
- Build a sincere-looking case or shaky claim; repeated self-nominations usually make the table refuse to hang you. (seen in 7 games)
