# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-13 13:02 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 375 active entries of tips_notebook.json (82 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules c6fe18800fc90afb.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of stated votes and announced totals; a stated vote that doesn't fit a total is usually one miscast ballot, a lead rather than a hang. (seen in 19 games)
- When totals don't fit stated votes, first ask voters to re-check their reminders; a Fixer, Ballot Stuffer (total exceeds voters) or Merciful/Zealot trait is rarer. If your Guilty seems to count Innocent, switch your next ballot. (seen in 9 games)
- Tokens die with you: spend one-shot tokens the first time they can swing a vote or remove a threat. Village kill shots usually need a real lead (a broken claim), not a claim clash or a 'test'. (seen in 23 games)
- An unexplained Katana survival needn't mean innate Defense: a hidden Shaky holder's attacks never kill, and hidden Narrow Escape saves anyone once. Yakuza whose attacks keep failing should move the Katana.
- Keep past results, tonight's planned target and what each outcome would mean in your will; the result from the night you die never reaches it. (seen in 48 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action; still, a corroborated lone unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is usually the attacker. (seen in 43 games)
- Check the rules before calling a contradiction: only Smuggler and Vulture are unique, so duplicate claimants can both be real and both deserve testing. (seen in 43 games)
- Keep votes consistent with stated reads and answer direct night questions in one plain sentence; unexplained switches and repeated evasion become cases themselves. (seen in 38 games)

## Village
- Read every cause of death and keep a Neutral-killer suspect list (quiet unclaimed players, unexplained survivors): unhunted Serial Killers and Arsonists often win, though near the vote lock the hang usually belongs on your best Yakuza lead. (seen in 13 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name a mechanical fit (a Yakuza-only claim, a broken claim, a group result leaving one name). (seen in 64 games)
- Count down to the vote lock: once Yakuza hold half the living players no nomination passes, so 'wait for tonight's results' is usually a stall; hang the best lead while you can. (seen in 18 games)
- Investigators: post night, target and exact result once it bears on a vote, but don't announce tonight's target or that you'll act; that usually gets you blocked or killed. (seen in 46 games)
- Avoid claiming or hinting at a power role on Days 1-2 without a result that matters: claimants are the usual Katana targets, and exact claims feed a Ritualist. (seen in 37 games)
- Once a mechanical result (including a public Yakuza-only role claim, even retracted) shows someone Yakuza, look next at who delayed their hang, defended them or PM'd them. (seen in 47 games)
- A player who self-nominates or pushes without data is often a Jester: prefer another hang, or keep Guilty votes to a bare majority since revenge hits a Guilty voter. (seen in 25 games)
- The Katana holder, Serial Killers and Arsonists all read Not Evil, so a clean Sheriff read clears little. (seen in 25 games)
- A Turncoat (a Villager who wins with the Yakuza) reads Evil but counts Good in a census, and hanging one doesn't lower the Yakuza count; Turncoats should vote with the town.
- Remorseful players die if they vote Guilty on a hanged true Villager, so they should vote Guilty only on corroborated cases. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village, so recheck who pushed it.

## Yakuza
- Doomed teammates usually get bused: unless vote math shows you can save them, vote Yes and Guilty openly, since stray Innocents and public defenses mark the partner. (seen in 56 games)
- Keep team talk in night chat: daytime PMs between teammates are visible to all and often become the town's main lead. (seen in 60 games)
- On Night 1 confirm holder and target (one missing vote can cancel the kill) and agree covers: Village roles fitting each member's tokens and visits, never a Yakuza-only role; switching claims when cornered usually hangs you. (seen in 41 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow your team, not someone whose result is already public; after a failed Katana, assume protection and remove or block the protector before retrying. (seen in 32 games)
- Smuggler teams: plan swaps in night chat. Aim the Katana at a decoy swapped with the Medic's likely ward, or self-swap to dodge a check; every action aimed at a swapped player moves, your own team's included. (seen in 5 games)
- Move the Katana off any member a visit result has named, and admit an exposed visit with a prepared cover rather than denying it. (seen in 26 games)

## Neutral
- Solo killers and Jesters do best with one committed, plain claim: quiet late claimants become the default hang, slow-igniting Arsonists usually hang first, and repeat self-nominating Jesters get refused. (seen in 15 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim) the first night you can, post it next Day, and don't announce tomorrow's exam. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Put free protection on likely Katana targets (claimed investigators, vocal leaders), keep wards secret, and spend tokens on Strong protection once a Serial Killer or Arsonist shows. (seen in 34 games)
