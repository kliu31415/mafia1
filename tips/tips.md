# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-13 10:28 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 367 active entries of tips_notebook.json (79 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules 14c4936724ba8b44.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret: keep a notepad of stated votes and announced totals. A mismatch is usually a miscast, so question it rather than hang on it. (seen in 18 games)
- If totals don't fit stated votes, first have voters re-check their reminders; suspect a Fixer or Ballot Stuffer only with a second sign. (seen in 9 games)
- Tokens die with you, so spend one-shot tokens the first time they can matter. Kill shots need a real lead, not a claim clash or a 'test', but shouldn't wait for proof. (seen in 20 games)
- A hidden Shaky Yakuza's Katana never kills, so an unexplained survival isn't always innate Defense. Yakuza: move the Katana if one member's attacks keep failing.
- A visit shows a destination, not an action. A corroborated lone visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof; one unverified report is only that claimant's word. (seen in 41 games)
- Only Smuggler and Vulture are unique, so a duplicate claimant is often real: test both before hanging or shooting either. (seen in 43 games)
- Keep past results and tonight's plan in your will; the result of the night you die never reaches it, so post what matters publicly. (seen in 45 games)
- Vote as you said you would and answer night-action questions in one plain sentence; unexplained switches and dodges usually become the case against you. (seen in 38 games)

## Village
- Before voting Guilty, name a mechanical fit (broken claim, corroborated visit, first-person slip), not a lean. But when no result is coming, hang the best lead rather than wait. (seen in 61 games)
- 'Wait for tonight's results' usually costs an investigator. Count to the vote lock: once the Yakuza hold half the living players, no nomination passes. (seen in 17 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers deliberately: read every cause of death and list quiet unclaimed players and unexplained survivors (a Not Evil read doesn't clear them). Near the lock, hang the Yakuza lead first. (seen in 22 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows someone Yakuza, suspect whoever argued to delay their hang, defended them or PM'd them; a Guilty vote on them clears nobody. (seen in 45 games)
- Investigators: post night, target and exact result once it bears on a vote; a held-back result looks retrofitted. (seen in 37 games)
- Avoid hinting at a power role early or announcing tonight's target: it usually draws the Katana, a block or a Ritualist guess. (seen in 45 games)
- A player who self-nominates or pushes with no data is often a Jester, who also reads Evil. Prefer another hang, or keep Guilty votes to a bare majority. (seen in 23 games)
- PM pairs who then push the same line are a lead: ask both publicly what was said, and convict only with an independent break. (seen in 40 games)
- A public claim of a Yakuza-only role, even 'retracted', is nearly a confession: hang that Day. (seen in 11 games)
- Survival confirms nothing: a Katana survivor with no named protector and a Not Evil read fits a Serial Killer. (seen in 24 games)
- A Turncoat reads Evil but counts Good in a census and wins with the Yakuza; hanging one doesn't lower the Yakuza count.
- Remorseful: your Guilty on a hanged Villager kills you, so vote Guilty only on corroborated cases. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village.

## Yakuza
- Keep team talk in night chat. Daytime PMs between teammates usually become the town's main lead. (seen in 57 games)
- Don't defend a doomed teammate: unless your votes can stop the hang, vote Yes and Guilty and say so. Stray Innocents show in the totals. (seen in 54 games)
- Confirm holder and target in night chat and all vote; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 40 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1: a Village role fitting your tokens and visits, not a Yakuza-only role. Switching claims when cornered is usually fatal. (seen in 29 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow your team (Census Taker, Oracle, Accountant, visit counters); near parity, kill the Vigilante or Sniper instead. (seen in 31 games)
- After a failed Katana, assume a protector or innate Defense: remove the likely Medic rather than retry. Fresh claimants are often guarded or trapped. (seen in 32 games)
- Move the Katana off a member a watcher has named; admit an exposed visit with a prepared cover rather than deny it. (seen in 26 games)
- Speak only from a Villager's view: slips like 'my Katana' or night-chat facts have hanged several Yakuza. (seen in 12 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, so a clean read on a suspected attacker clears nothing. (seen in 24 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim) on the first night possible; don't announce your next target. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard likely Katana targets (claimed investigators, hang leaders) and don't announce wards. Free protection doesn't stop a Strong attack, so spend a token once a Neutral killer shows. (seen in 34 games)

## Smuggler
- A swap moves every action aimed at either player: swap a decoy Katana target with the guarded one, or self-swap when expecting a check or shot. Both players are told, so prepare a cover. (seen in 14 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once three players are doused, or two if you're being questioned; Arsonists who keep dousing usually hang first. (seen in 14 games)
