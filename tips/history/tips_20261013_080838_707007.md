# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-13 08:08 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 352 active entries of tips_notebook.json (77 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of every stated vote and announced total; a mismatch is a lead to question, rarely a hang by itself. (seen in 18 games)
- When totals don't fit stated votes, a miscast is likelier than a Fixer: have voters re-check their reminders first. A total above the voter count does prove a Ballot Stuffer. (seen in 9 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens the first time they can swing a vote or remove a threat; they die with you. Kill shots usually need a real lead, not a claim clash or a 'test'. (seen in 19 games)
- Vote as you said you would, or explain the switch first; unexplained mismatches get cited against you. (seen in 38 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action. A corroborated lone visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof; one unverified report is only a claim. (seen in 40 games)
- Check the rules before calling a contradiction: only Smuggler and Vulture are unique, so duplicate claimants can both be real. (seen in 42 games)
- Keep past results and tonight's plan in your will, and post key results publicly: the result from the night you die never reaches it. (seen in 43 games)
- Answer questions about your night action in one plain sentence; repeated evasion tends to become the case against you. (seen in 19 games)

## Village
- Investigators: post night, target and exact result once it bears on a vote; held-back results look retrofitted and often die with you. (seen in 36 games)
- Avoid hinting at your role or announcing tonight's target, even by PM; players who do are usually blocked or killed that night. (seen in 44 games)
- Convict on mechanical cases (a Yakuza-only role claim, a broken claim, tokens contradicting a claim), not leans. 'Wait for results' is often a Yakuza stall: hang your best case before they hold half the votes. (seen in 59 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows a player Yakuza, suspect whoever defended them, argued delay or PM'd them; a mere liar may be a Jester. (seen in 44 games)
- A pair who PM daily and vote alike is a lead: ask each publicly what was said, and convict once an independent break appears. (seen in 39 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers on purpose: read every cause of death and list late Vanilla claimants and unexplained survivors, whom a Not Evil read doesn't clear. Near the vote lock, hang the Yakuza lead first, and don't spend hangs on Neutrals that never block wins. (seen in 12 games)
- Someone who self-nominates, pushes without data or reads Evil yet invites the rope is often a Jester: prefer sparing them, or keep Guilty votes to a bare majority. (seen in 22 games)
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil, so a clean Sheriff read clears little, and a lone last Yakuza reads clean every night. (seen in 23 games)

## Yakuza
- Plan in night chat, confirming holder and target every night, rather than by daytime PMs; a visible teammate PM pair often becomes the town's main lead. (seen in 56 games)
- Defending a doomed teammate rarely pays, since totals are compared with stated votes; unless your votes can stop the hang, vote Guilty and say so. (seen in 53 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1 that fit your tokens and real visits, avoid Yakuza-only role names, and don't switch when cornered; audits and exact-role checks break improvised claims. (seen in 29 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow your team (Census Taker, Oracle, Accountant, a working blocker); near parity, a Vigilante or Sniper with shots comes first. (seen in 29 games)
- After a failed Katana, assume a protector or innate Defense; remove the protector or push the survivor's hang rather than retrying. (seen in 31 games)
- Give the Katana to an expendable member unlikely to be watched or blocked, and move it once a visit result names the holder. (seen in 26 games)
- Use rigs, puppets and other one-shot tokens early, since holders usually die with them, but not a rig on a Day a teammate may be nominated. (seen in 25 games)
- Smuggler teams: plan swaps rather than fearing them. A swap moves every action aimed at either player: aim the Katana at a decoy swapped with the Medic's likely ward, or self-swap to dodge a check or shot; both players are told. (seen in 13 games)
- Speak only from your cover: 'my Katana' slips and flat denials of a visit a log later shows tend to hang you. (seen in 19 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (a duplicate claim, a hanged suspect) on the first night possible, and post night, target and role the next Day without announcing your next exam. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard likely Katana targets (loud analysts, then claimed investigators) without naming your ward. Standard stops the Katana but not a Serial Killer or ignite, so spend a token on Strong once one appears. (seen in 33 games)

## Vigilante
- Set a trigger by Day 2-3 and fire a Basic shot on a lead while the Village has numbers; dying with unused shots is the usual failure. (seen in 17 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once three players are doused, or two if you're being questioned; Arsonists who keep dousing usually hang with nothing burned. (seen in 14 games)
