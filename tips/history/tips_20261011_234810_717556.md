# Tips from past games

## Everyone
- Before voting Guilty, name one concrete mechanical fit: an Evil result against a Vanilla claim, a corroborated lone visitor to a Katana victim, a group result whose other members are confirmed, or a switched claim. A role-block fully explains a missing result. But if one more kill would let the Yakuza block every nomination, it usually pays to hang the best case today. (seen in 44 games)
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of each player's stated vote on every nomination and trial, the announced totals and your own vote. More Guilty votes than stated Guilty usually means someone hid a vote, so quote the notepad when stories shift. (seen in 11 games)
- Totals can be altered on purpose: a Ballot Stuffer's trial vote counts twice, and a Fixer secretly flips one player's trial votes. When totals don't fit, first have everyone recheck their private reminder, since misposts are the commonest cause. Nomination Yes counts can't be altered, so they are the cleanest test for a liar. (seen in 5 games)
- Tokens die with you. Spend one-shot tokens on the first Day or night they can swing a vote or remove a threat. A puppet or doubled ballot lapses if you die that night, so spend it a night early. (seen in 8 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action. Still, a corroborated lone unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is close to proof, especially if they first denied the visit. (seen in 34 games)
- Keep tonight's plan in your will and post results publicly by day. A result from the night you die never reaches your will. (seen in 32 games)
- Keep your votes matched to your stated reads and explain any switch beforehand. Answer questions about your night action plainly the first time, because evasion tends to become its own case. (seen in 32 games)

## Village
- Post your role, target and exact result in your first speech after getting it. If someone copies your claim, counterclaim at once with your full log. Results held back tend to look retrofitted. (seen in 23 games)
- Once a player is shown Yakuza by a mechanical result, look next at who defended them, urged delay or PM'd them. Check those players' stated votes against the totals. (seen in 35 games)
- Keep tonight's target secret, in public and in PMs, and assume a hidden Wiretap reads every PM. A PM link is a lead to check, usually not grounds for a hang. (seen in 30 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers on purpose. A Serial Killer cause of death or a multi-death ignite proves one is alive, so keep a list of unwatched Vanilla claimants and unexplained survivors. While the Katana is still killing, avoid spending hangs on Neutrals that never block your win (Jester, Executioner, Bounty Hunter, Magpie). (seen in 3 games)
- Before a hang, ask whether the nominee wins by being hanged. Eager self-nominators and easily disproved lone claims are often Jesters. If you must hang one, aim for a bare Guilty majority and keep confirmed power roles on Innocent, since the revenge hits a random Guilty voter. (seen in 16 games)
- On Day 2, avoid hinting at protective roles or tokens. While a Ritualist may be alive, don't name other players' exact roles in public. (seen in 24 games)

## Yakuza
- Don't defend a doomed teammate, because the totals expose hidden defensive votes. Do the vote math in night chat. If your votes can't save them, vote Guilty and state the vote you actually cast. (seen in 45 games)
- Avoid daytime PMs to teammates, above all on Day 2 or when one of you is under pressure. Plan in night chat. (seen in 39 games)
- Before Day 2, agree a Village claim for each member that matches their tokens and real visits. Avoid Unique roles and anything Yakuza-only. Switching claims after one breaks usually hangs you faster. (seen in 23 games)
- Every member should submit the agreed holder and target every night. With two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 26 games)
- Don't flatly deny a visit that a watch, track or count could expose. Admit it with a cover prepared in night chat, and move the Katana once the holder is narrowed down. (seen in 17 games)
- After a failed kill, assume protection or innate Defense. Remove the protector rather than retrying the same target. (seen in 23 games)
- Use rigs, puppets and cleans early. Rig a Day when a non-teammate is the likely nominee, never your own. (seen in 22 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, so add that caveat when you post a Not Evil. A Not Evil player still usually ranks below unchecked players as a suspect, unless only one Yakuza can remain. (seen in 20 games)

## Coroner
- Examine bodies that settle a live question, such as a hanged suspect or one side of a claim duel, not undisputed Katana victims. Post night, target and role at once, and don't announce your next exam. (seen in 3 games)

## Medic
- Use free Standard protection on vocal analysts and claimed investigators, and vary your targets. Once you claim Medic, spend a token on self-protection within a night or two. You never learn whether a save happened, so never claim one. (seen in 27 games)

## Vigilante
- Dying with unused shots is the usual failure. Set a trigger by Day 3, such as a claim conflict or an Evil read on an unverifiable claim, and fire a Basic shot once it is met. (seen in 13 games)

## Smuggler
- Plan swaps in night chat rather than vetoing them. To beat the Medic, aim the Katana at a decoy and swap the decoy with your real target. To dodge a check, swap yourself with a Villager. Both swapped players are told, so have a cover for your visits. (seen in 2 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once 3 living players are doused, or 2 if you're being questioned. Each extra night spent dousing gives the town time to hang you. (seen in 12 games)
