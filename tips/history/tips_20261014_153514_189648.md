# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-14 15:35 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 378 active entries of tips_notebook.json (87 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules ec372e72ebc79cea.

## Everyone
- Convict on a named mechanical break: a broken or Yakuza-only claim, a first-person slip, a corroborated lone visitor to a victim, or a group result whose other members are confirmed. Stacked leans and wording don't count. Before voting Guilty, ask whether the nominee wins by being hanged (Jester or Executioner). (seen in 68 games)
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad: each player's stated Yes/No and Guilty/Innocent, the announced totals and your own vote. A stated vote that the totals contradict is usually a miscast. Ask about it once and follow it as a lead, but it is rarely enough to hang on. (seen in 21 games)
- When totals don't fit, first have every voter re-check their reminder and say how their vote counted (the Merciful and Zealot traits change it). If the gap survives, it is real. More votes than voters means a Ballot Stuffer. One extra Guilty on a Village claimant's trial often means a Fixer flipped the nominee's own Innocent, so look at who pushed the hang. Yes counts can't be altered. (seen in 10 games)
- Only the Vulture is unique, so two claimants of the same role can both be real. Test both with known-visit targets, exact-role checks and counts rather than hanging or shooting on the clash. (seen in 47 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not what the visitor did. A lone visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof only when a second source backs it up. One unverified claimant's track or map is only a claim. (seen in 46 games)
- Write tonight's plan into your will, with what each possible result would mean. A result from the night you die never reaches your will, so post important results while you are alive. (seen in 53 games)
- Answer 'what did you do last night?' in one plain sentence the first time you are asked. Days of evasion become the case against you. (seen in 22 games)
- An unexplained survival or attack notice proves little. Possible causes: innate Defense (Serial Killer, Arsonist, Jester), the hidden Narrow Escape trait (works once), a Shaky holder whose Katana never kills (if one Yakuza's attacks keep failing, move the Katana), or a Paranoid villager's made-up notice. A Villager's Stolen Blade attack also reads 'Katana'. (seen in 25 games)
- Tokens die with you, so spend one-shot tokens (cleans, fixes, Medic tokens, guesses) the first time they matter. Kill tokens need a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits. A claim clash, a 'test' of an untested Medic, or a Neutral that never blocks wins isn't enough. At parity, fire even on a coin flip. (seen in 28 games)

## Village
- Post a result that bears on a vote right away: night, target, exact result. A result held back reads as retrofitted. An exact-role claim with nothing to decide just feeds a possible Ritualist, so keep that in your will instead. (seen in 39 games)
- Don't hint at your role on Days 1-2 or announce tonight's target, even by PM, because a Wiretap may be reading. The loudest Day 1 organizer is the usual Night 1 kill. (seen in 50 games)
- Once someone is shown to be Yakuza by a mechanical result, their defenders, PM partners and any hidden No or Innocent in the totals are your next leads. Someone merely caught lying may be a Jester, so don't chain from them. (seen in 49 games)
- Each morning, count down to the vote lock: once the Yakuza are half the living players, they block every nomination (with 2 Yakuza, that is 4 alive). On the Day before the lock, hang your best Yakuza lead even on a 50/50, and refuse 'wait for tonight's result' pitches, because the investigator usually dies first. (seen in 20 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers on purpose. Read every cause of death (a Serial Killer or ignite cause proves one is alive), list unclaimed claimants and unexplained survivors, and aim checks there. A Not Evil read doesn't clear them. Hanging one doesn't lower the Yakuza count, so near the lock the hang goes to the Yakuza lead. (seen in 16 games)
- A likely Jester (self-nominates, begs for the rope, tells reckless lies) is usually best left alive. If you must hang one, agree on a bare Guilty majority so the revenge has few targets, and have confirmed power roles vote Innocent. (seen in 26 games)
- A Turncoat (at most one per game) reads Evil to a Sheriff or Seer, counts as Good in a census, and wins with the Yakuza. Hanging one doesn't lower the Yakuza count. If you are the Turncoat, vote with the town and help the Yakuza only where it won't mark you.
- If you are Remorseful, a Guilty vote on a Villager who gets hanged kills you too. Vote Guilty only on a corroborated case, and otherwise declare an 'insurance Innocent'. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village.
- Sheriff: the Katana holder reads Not Evil, and once only one Yakuza is left, a Not Evil read clears nobody. An Evil read also fits a Jester, Executioner, Notorious villager, Turncoat or framed player. (seen in 25 games)

## Yakuza
- Teammates shouldn't PM each other by day; plan in night chat. Visible PM pairs are the town's favourite lead, and an Eavesdropper can publish what you wrote. (seen in 65 games)
- Only defend a doomed teammate if the team's votes can actually save them. Otherwise vote Guilty and say so, because a hidden Innocent shows up in the totals. (seen in 58 games)
- Every member should submit the agreed Katana holder, target and action every night. With two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 41 games)
- Agree covers in Night 1 chat that fit your tokens and your real visits. Don't claim Vanilla or a Yakuza-only role, and don't switch claims once yours is broken. (seen in 32 games)
- Kill whoever's next result threatens you most (Census Taker, counters, exact-role checkers, blockers) before investigators who have already posted. After a failed Katana attack, assume protection or Defense, then switch targets or remove the protector. (seen in 34 games)

## Medic
- Use your free Standard protection on likely Katana targets, such as claimed investigators. It doesn't stop Strong attacks (Serial Killer, ignite, Katana Expert). Don't visit a possible Veteran, and spend your tokens before you die. (seen in 35 games)
