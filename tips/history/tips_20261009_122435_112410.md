# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-09 12:22 by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 39 active entries of tips_notebook.json (3 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier versions are in history/.

## Everyone
- Keep a running ledger of public facts: every nomination and trial vote, each private message with its sender and recipient, each claimed result, and each flip. Check every accusation against it before you make it or believe it, because one reversed fact discredits the accuser. (seen in 3 games)
- Weigh contradictions between a player's own stated premises above awkward wording or a slow answer. Delays can come from framing, Roleplayers or Executioner pushes, so ask which premise they reject, and settle duplicate claims with visit or investigation evidence. (seen in 2 games)
- A visit shows where someone went, not what they did. The Katana holder visits the victim, so a Lookout watching the holder sees no visitors, and a night kill on an attacker doesn't cancel their attack. Check which roles fit a result before calling it contradictory. (seen in 2 games)
- Keep your votes consistent with your stated reads, and hunt for players who accuse someone and then vote No or Innocent on them. Vigilantes have repeatedly shot Yakuza for exactly this.
- Each day, ask every living player for their top suspect and a one-sentence reason. Treat repeated 'no case, no nomination' and a sudden pivot after days of silence as signals worth probing. (seen in 2 games)
- A survived Katana attack, or repeated role-blocks on a claimant, suggests their power role is real. A missing result alone is not evidence of lying, since a blocked investigator gets nothing.
- Everyone sees who messages whom, so briefly explain your private messages in public. Unexplained PM clusters become the town's main lead against you.

## Village
- As an investigator, post your target and result in your first speech after getting it, and keep your last will updated with every result. Holding a result back to bait a counterclaim makes your later reveal look invented, and that got a real Sheriff hanged.
- When two Sheriff claimants call each other Evil, both can be telling the truth, because Jesters and Executioners read Evil. Don't hang either one over who sounded better. Wait a day for mechanical evidence.
- Don't publicly announce future night targets. Blockers and killers will use the information. Tell one trusted player privately, then post the result at dawn.
- Don't hint at a power role on Day 1. Players who mentioned Medic tokens were killed Night 1. (seen in 2 games)
- Before a dangerous night, put your strongest contradiction into your will. Wills only update at the start of the day, so share later finds publicly or with a trusted player promptly.
- Keep the Serial Killer and Arsonist in the suspect pool after the Yakuza die. A Not Evil result or a quiet Vanilla claim clears no one.
- In the endgame, count the living factions before each vote. Weigh hard links to flipped Yakuza, such as shared Innocent votes and PMs from them, above convenient uncheckable claims like Roleplayer. (seen in 2 games)

## Yakuza
- Every member must submit the agreed Katana holder and target every night, and you should restate both in night chat. One missing vote cancels the kill.
- While a Lookout or Tracker is active, give the Katana to a member with no claimed role. Kill the likely Vigilante first, since each of their shots costs you more than any info role. (seen in 2 games)
- Don't fake-claim a role a living holder or watcher can contradict, especially Medic. Prefer Vanilla, or a claim built on your team's own frames.
- Don't DM teammates on Day 1 or stack Guilty votes together on weak pushes. Once one of you flips, PM triangles and shared vote records hang the rest.
- After a teammate flips, vote with the town and don't immediately push the villagers who opposed them.
- Never speak from your hidden view in public: say 'the Katana kill', not 'my Katana'.

## Neutral
- Give specific reads and a believable soft claim early. A no-info Vanilla claim becomes the default hang once Village roles confirm each other.

## Tracker
- Track players who claim night actions so a mismatch is informative, and cross-check your results against Lookout claims.

## Lookout
- Watch exposed investigators and likely kill targets to catch both the attacker and the protector. Repeating a watch on a quiet player tells you little.

## Medic
- Never skip Night 1, because Standard Defense stops the Katana. Protect vocal analysts and claimed investigators, and save a token for a Strong protection. You get no feedback, so never claim you saw a save. (seen in 2 games)

## Vigilante
- Stay hidden and never announce your shots. Use Basic shots on players whose votes contradict their reads, and claim only once your flips confirm you. (seen in 2 games)

## Arsonist
- Douse players likely to survive, count the living each night, and ignite once your douses would swing the endgame. Waiting too long got an Arsonist hanged.

## Executioner
- Claim Sheriff early with an Evil result on your target and one simple reason. Village players: this is why a real-looking counterclaim needs mechanical checking.
