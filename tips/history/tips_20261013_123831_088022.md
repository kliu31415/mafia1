# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-13 12:38 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 375 active entries of tips_notebook.json (82 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules 14c4936724ba8b44.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of every stated vote and announced total, and cast the vote you stated. A stated vote that doesn't fit a total is a lead to question, rarely grounds to hang. (seen in 38 games)
- When totals don't fit stated votes, first have voters re-check their reminders: a miscast or lie is far likelier than a Fixer or Ballot Stuffer. Nomination votes can't be altered, so a mismatch there means a liar. (seen in 9 games)
- Tokens die with you, so spend one-shot tokens the first time they can swing a vote or remove a threat. Kill tokens need a lead first (a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits, not a claim clash), though at parity even a coin-flip shot beats holding. (seen in 23 games)
- Before each night, write past results, tonight's plan and how to read each outcome in your will; the result of the night you die never reaches it. (seen in 48 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action, and players can make several visits; a lone unexplained visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof only when the report is corroborated. (seen in 43 games)
- Only Smuggler and Vulture are unique, so a duplicate claim proves no lie, yet backs neither claimant: test both. (seen in 43 games)
- One unexplained survival proves little: it fits a Medic, innate Defense, hidden Narrow Escape or a Shaky Katana holder, whose attacks never kill. Failures on several targets suggest Shaky (Yakuza: move the Katana); a repeat failure on one player suggests real Defense. (seen in 25 games)

## Village
- Post a result (night, target, exact wording) once it bears on a vote, but early role hints or announcing tonight's target usually get you blocked or killed; PMs may be read, and exact claims feed a Ritualist. (seen in 46 games)
- Convict on a mechanical fit (a Yakuza-only role claim, a first-person attack slip, a corroborated lone visit, a token count that breaks a claim), not on leans or wording. (seen in 64 games)
- Count the vote lock: once the Yakuza hold half the living votes no nomination passes, so 'wait for tonight's results' often loses. Hang the best available lead before then. (seen in 18 games)
- Once someone is shown Yakuza mechanically, those who argued delay, stated Innocent or traded PMs with them are usually the next leads; ask both ends of a PM pair publicly what was said. (seen in 47 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers: a Serial Killer or Arsonist cause of death, or an unexplained multi-death night, points to one, so list unclaimed players and unexplained survivors; a Not Evil read doesn't clear them. Near the lock the hang usually belongs on the Yakuza lead, and Neutrals that never block wins aren't worth one. (seen in 13 games)
- Self-nominators and evidence-free pushers are often Jesters: prefer not to hang them, or keep Guilty votes to a bare majority with power roles voting Innocent, since revenge hits a random Guilty voter. (seen in 25 games)
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil, while Evil fits a Yakuza, Jester, Executioner, framed or Notorious player, or a Turncoat (a Villager who wins with the Yakuza yet counts Good in a census). Hanging a Turncoat doesn't cut the Yakuza count; Turncoats should vote with the town and read kills to find their side. (seen in 25 games)
- If Remorseful, a Guilty vote on a hanged Villager kills you, so vote Guilty only on corroborated cases. A 'remorse' death proves hanged player and voter were Village: re-examine whoever built that case.

## Yakuza
- Plan only in night chat: daytime teammate PMs are visible to everyone and often become the town's main lead. (seen in 60 games)
- Defend a doomed teammate only if your votes can actually stop the hang; otherwise vote Guilty and say so, because totals expose hidden Innocents. (seen in 56 games)
- Confirm holder and target in night chat and submit every night; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 41 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1: a Village role fitting your tokens and visits. Naming a Yakuza-only role or switching claims when cornered is usually fatal. (seen in 30 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow your team; after a failed attack, remove the likely protector rather than retrying. (seen in 32 games)

## Neutral
- Solo killers: fix one tokenless cover early and keep it; Arsonists usually do best igniting at about three douses, before questions grow. (seen in 15 games)

## Coroner
- Examine bodies that settle a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim) on the first night possible, post night, target and role next Day, and don't announce targets. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Free Standard protection on the likeliest Katana target often carries the town, but only a token's Strong protection stops a Neutral killer; don't announce wards. (seen in 34 games)

## Smuggler
- A swap moves every action aimed at either swapped player, so plan it in night chat: swap a Katana decoy with the Medic's likely ward, or a suspected teammate with a Villager to dodge checks. Both players are told, so Villagers should post swap notices. (seen in 14 games)
