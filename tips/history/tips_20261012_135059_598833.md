# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-12 13:50 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 340 active entries of tips_notebook.json (73 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of announced totals and each player's stated vote. A stated vote that doesn't fit the totals is usually a miscast: a lead, rarely a hang by itself. (seen in 16 games)
- Totals can be rigged: a Ballot Stuffer adds a vote and a Fixer silently flips one player's trial votes all Day. Have everyone re-check their vote reminder first; if your own vote counts backwards, say so and switch it. (seen in 8 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens the first time they can change a vote or remove a threat; holders often die with them unused. Kill shots usually need a lead first, not a claim clash: a second claimant of your role may be real. (seen in 17 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action: a corroborated lone visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof, but one unverified claimant's report is only their word. (seen in 40 games)
- Check the role text before calling a contradiction: only Smuggler and Vulture are unique, actions are optional, and a role-block fully explains a missing result. (seen in 40 games)
- Put past results and tonight's plan in your will nightly; the result of the night you die never reaches it, so post important results while alive. (seen in 39 games)
- Keep votes consistent with your stated reads, and answer questions about your night action plainly the first time; unexplained switches and repeated evasion often become the whole case. (seen in 37 games)

## Village
- Before voting Guilty, name a mechanical fit (broken claim, Yakuza-only role claim, first-person attack slip, corroborated visit), not wording or stacked leans. If no result is coming or the vote lock is near, hang the best lead available. (seen in 56 games)
- Keep tonight's target, and whether you'll act at all, out of public chat and PMs (a Wiretap may read them); announced actors tend to be blocked or killed that night. (seen in 42 games)
- Investigators: post night, target and exact result as soon as it bears on a vote; held-back results look retrofitted. With no result, claiming on Day 1-2 mostly just draws the night kill. (seen in 33 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows a player Yakuza, suspect whoever defended them, delayed their hang or PM'd them, and compare stated votes with totals for hidden Innocents. A caught liar alone may be a Jester. (seen in 41 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers deliberately: read every cause of death and list late-Vanilla players and unexplained Katana survivors, whom Not Evil reads don't clear. Hang the best suspect before the endgame, but rarely ahead of a stronger Yakuza case or just before Yakuza reach half the votes. (seen in 10 games)
- A player who self-nominates, pushes without data or reads Evil yet invites the rope is often a Jester. If you hang anyway, keep Guilty votes to a bare majority: the revenge kills a random Guilty voter. (seen in 21 games)
- "Wait for tonight's results" is a common Yakuza line: each no-hang Day costs a kill, often the investigator you awaited. Count the deaths left before the Yakuza hold half the votes. (seen in 14 games)

## Yakuza
- Teammates who PM each other by Day, vote alike and explain vaguely tend to become the town's main lead; plan in night chat instead. (seen in 53 games)
- Defend a doomed teammate only if your votes can actually stop the hang; otherwise vote Guilty and say so, because totals expose hidden Innocents. (seen in 50 games)
- Confirm holder, target and every action in night chat each night; with two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 36 games)
- Agree Village covers on Night 1 that fit your tokens and real visits, since audits, exact-role checks and real holders test them. Naming a Yakuza-only role or switching claims when cornered usually hangs you. (seen in 28 games)
- After a failed Katana, assume a protector or innate Defense: remove the protector, or push the survivor to the gallows. (seen in 30 games)
- Kill whoever's next result could narrow your team (Census Taker, Oracle, Accountant, a blocker), not someone whose results are already public; near parity, a Sniper or Vigilante with shots comes first. (seen in 25 games)
- Keep the Katana on an unexposed member you can afford to lose (the holder reads Not Evil to Sheriffs), moving it once a visit result names the holder. (seen in 24 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (one side of a duplicate claim, a hanged suspect) the first night it exists, then post night, target and role; announcing tomorrow's exam marks you for the kill. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Put free Standard protection on likely Katana targets (claimed investigators), unclaimed and with your ward unnamed. It doesn't stop Strong attacks, so spend a token once a Serial Killer or Arsonist shows. (seen in 32 games)

## Smuggler
- Teams often wrongly veto swaps: a swap moves exactly the actions aimed at the two swapped players. Aim the Katana at a decoy swapped with the Medic's likely ward, or self-swap with a Villager to dodge a check. (seen in 3 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once three living players are doused, or two if you're being questioned; every extra dousing night lets the town hang you first. (seen in 13 games)
