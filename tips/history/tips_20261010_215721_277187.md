# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-10 21:56 by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 245 active entries of tips_notebook.json (46 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of every nomination's Yes count, every trial's totals and each player's stated vote. More No or Innocent votes than players who said so means someone hid a defensive vote. (seen in 27 games)
- State your vote before each ballot and keep it matching your stated read; explain any switch before voting. A read/vote mismatch is a lead, never a hang on its own. (seen in 30 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name one mechanical case: an Evil result against a Vanilla claim, a corroborated lone visitor to a Katana victim, a group elimination, or tokens on a tokenless claim. A role-block fully explains a missing result, and behaviour-only or self-nomination cases need the Jester check first. (seen in 32 games)
- A visit shows a destination, not an action. Still, if a credible Lookout names X as the only unexplained visitor to a Katana victim, treat it as near-proof and don't let 'a visit isn't an attack' acquit X. (seen in 29 games)
- Check the rules before calling a contradiction: actions are optional, non-unique roles can repeat, and on Day 2 only Night 1 has passed. Equally, 'roles can repeat' only means a claim isn't disproved; it is not evidence for it. (seen in 28 games)
- Write tonight's plan and all past results into your will, labelled by night. Post important results publicly by day, because the result of the night you die never reaches your will. (seen in 27 games)
- Answer a direct question about your night action in one plain sentence the first time. Repeated evasion becomes a case by itself. (seen in 7 games)

## Village
- Investigators: post role, night, target and exact result in your first speech after getting it, and counterclaim at once with your full log if someone copies you. Withholding protects nothing and looks retrofitted later. (seen in 16 games)
- Don't hint at a power role on Day 1. Never announce tonight's target, publicly or by PM: the Pharmacist blocks it and the Katana routes around it. (seen in 24 games)
- A PM link is a lead, not a hang. Jesters and Executioners leave PM trails on purpose, so require a mechanical result or stated votes that the totals back up. (seen in 28 games)
- Once someone is mechanically shown Yakuza, suspect whoever argued to delay their hang or defended them. Voting No at the nomination and then Guilty once the hang is certain is the telling pattern. (seen in 27 games)
- Before any hang, ask whether the nominee wins by being hanged: eager to be tried, an easily disproved claim, a self-nomination. If you must hang a likely Jester, keep Guilty to a bare majority with confirmed power roles voting Innocent. (seen in 15 games)
- Keep Serial Killers and Arsonists in the suspect pool all game. Read every cause of death, press unclaimed players who admit an unnamed action, and expect an Arsonist's multi-kill night. (seen in 17 games)
- Surviving an attack confirms nothing: SK and Arsonist have Standard Defense, Jester and Executioner Basic. Ask the survivor at once who protected them. (seen in 15 games)
- An archive is a sample: about a quarter of the entries are fakes, and roles can repeat. Use it as odds to decide whom to press, audit or watch, not as proof of a liar. (seen in 14 games)
- Estimate the Yakuza count (about 3 in 11) and, late in the game, count how many more mis-hangs you can afford. Post all information before the Yakuza are one kill from a majority. (seen in 8 games)

## Yakuza
- Don't defend a doomed teammate: the totals expose hidden Innocent votes. Do the vote math in night chat, and if you can't save them, vote with the town. On a likely Jester, keep some of you on Innocent to stay out of the revenge draw. (seen in 34 games)
- Never PM teammates by day; an Eavesdropper or Wiretap reads plans word for word. Coordinate only in night chat. (seen in 30 games)
- Every Yakuza must submit the agreed Katana holder and target every night. One missing vote with two alive cancels the kill. (seen in 19 games)
- Give the Katana to your least valuable member, and move it the same night a Lookout or Tracker names the holder at a victim. (seen in 21 games)
- Fake-claim only a Village role whose token count matches yours and whose holder can't counterclaim. Never claim a Yakuza-only role, even as a hedge, and never switch claims once cornered. (seen in 18 games)
- After a failed kill, don't retry blindly. Kill or block the protector first, or hang an innate-Defense survivor by day. (seen in 19 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens (rig, puppet, swaps, clean) early, while they can swing a vote. Holders who wait for the decisive day die holding them. (seen in 18 games)
- Never flatly deny a visit a watcher could have seen. Agree a cover action before Day and admit the visit with it at once. (seen in 15 games)
- Never speak from night-chat knowledge, such as who held the Katana or who was blocked. If you slip, commit at once to a Village role that explains it. (seen in 7 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil, so a Not Evil result on a suspected attacker clears nothing; say so when posting. In the endgame, rely on the vote ledger instead. (seen in 16 games)

## Fortune Teller
- Post both raw totals by night every Day from Day 2 and line them up against claimed visits. On a Katana night, a visitor total of 1 makes the lone visitor the holder. (seen in 8 games)

## Medic
- Free Standard protection on vocal analysts and claimed investigators carries the town; vary the pattern, and self-protect with a token the night you claim. You get no feedback, so never claim a confirmed save. (seen in 21 games)

## Vigilante
- Dying with unused tokens is the usual failure. Fire a 1-token Basic shot on a real lead by Day 2-3, and post it if the shot fails on an unprotected suspect, since that shows innate Defense. (seen in 11 games)

## Clerk
- Rig a Day when a townie can win a nomination majority but not a Guilty majority. Never rig when a teammate is the likely nominee. Village: after an announced rig, suspect the nominator and anyone pushing that nomination. (seen in 18 games)

## Arsonist
- Ignite once 3 players are doused, or 2 if you're being questioned. Igniting is Astral, so traps, watchers and FT counts can't see it. (seen in 12 games)

## Jester
- Don't play clean: build a shaky but committed case or claim by midgame. Avoid repeated self-nominations, which make the town refuse every trial. (seen in 5 games)
