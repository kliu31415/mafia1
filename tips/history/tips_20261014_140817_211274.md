# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-14 14:08 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 394 active entries of tips_notebook.json (86 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules c6fe18800fc90afb.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad: each player's stated Yes/No and Guilty/Innocent, every Yes count and trial total, and each night's deaths and causes. A stated vote that doesn't fit a small total is usually a miscast. Ask that player once to re-check their reminder, and don't hang on the mismatch alone. Ask for stated votes when a vote is coming, not in every message. (seen in 21 games)
- If the gap is still there after the reminder check, someone manipulated the count. A total above the number of voters means a Ballot Stuffer. One extra Guilty on a Village claimant's trial usually means a Fixer flipped the nominee's own Innocent: read the nominee as likelier Village and look at who pushed hardest. Merciful/Zealot traits also move counts. Yes counts can't be altered, which makes them the cleanest test for a liar. (seen in 10 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens the first time they can change a vote or remove a threat, because tokens die with you. Kill tokens (shots, hidden gun, Stolen Blade, release) are the exception: they need a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits. A claim clash, a settled lead or a 'test' of a Medic's announced protection isn't one. At parity, fire anyway. (seen in 27 games)
- Only Vulture is Unique, so two claimants of any other role can both be real. Test both (visits a Lookout can see, exact-role checks, token audits) rather than hanging on the clash. A visit shows where someone went, not what they did, but a corroborated lone visitor to a Katana victim is close to proof. (seen in 46 games)
- Answer a direct question about your night action in one plain sentence the first time. Evasion stretched over Days becomes a case by itself. (seen in 22 games)
- A Katana survival with no protector named has many causes: innate Defense (SK/Arsonist), hidden Narrow Escape (once), a Shaky holder whose Katana never kills, a Villager's Stolen Blade that looks like the Katana, or a Paranoid fake notice. If several targets survive on different nights with no blocker, suspect one Shaky holder before hanging the survivors.
- A Turncoat is a Villager who wins with the Yakuza. They read Evil to checks but count Good in a census, and hanging one doesn't lower the Yakuza count. A Remorseful voter dies if they vote Guilty and the hanged player was truly Village. Remorseful players should vote Guilty only on corroborated cases. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village.

## Village
- Before voting Guilty, name one concrete mechanical break: a claimed Yakuza-only role, a first-person slip, a token count against a tokenless claim, a corroborated lone visitor, or a group that elimination narrows to one name. Echoed leans don't count. Near the vote lock, though, hang the best case available. (seen in 67 games)
- Count down to the lock each morning. Once the Yakuza hold half the living players they can vote down every nomination (2 Yakuza at 4 alive, 3 at 6). The Day before the lock is your last real hang, so spend it on a Yakuza lead, not a Neutral. (seen in 13 games)
- Investigators: post night, target and exact result in your first speech once it bears on a vote. Name only as much of your role as the result needs, because a Ritualist's guess kills through any Medic. Keep tonight's plan, and what each outcome would mean, in your will. (seen in 52 games)
- Never announce tonight's target or that you'll act, publicly or by PM. A Wiretap may read every PM, and announced targets get blocked or killed. Don't hint at a power role on Days 1-2 either. (seen in 49 games)
- Once someone is shown Yakuza mechanically, look next at who argued for delay, defended them or PM'd them. A player merely caught lying, or read Evil by one Sheriff, may be a Jester. Signs of a Jester: they push with no data, nominate themself, seem eager for the rope. Leave them alive, or keep Guilty votes to a bare majority. (seen in 49 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers on purpose. Read every cause of death: a Serial Killer or ignite cause proves that killer is alive, and a multi-death night suggests an ignite. Keep a suspect list of unwatched quiet claimants and of survivors no protector explains. A Not Evil read doesn't clear them, and at two alive you can't hang them. (seen in 15 games)
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night. With one Yakuza left, Not Evil clears nobody. (seen in 25 games)

## Yakuza
- Teammates usually shouldn't PM each other by Day. Everyone sees the PM, and the town asks both sides what it said. Plan only in night chat. (seen in 64 games)
- Don't defend a doomed teammate unless the team's votes can actually save them. Vote with the town and state the vote you really cast. Hidden Innocents show up in the totals. (seen in 58 games)
- Every member submits the agreed Katana holder and target every night. With two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 41 games)
- Agree covers on Night 1. Each should be a Village role that fits your tokens and real visits, and two members shouldn't share one. Never claim a Yakuza-only role and never speak from night-chat knowledge ('my Katana'). (seen in 32 games)
- Kill whoever's next result would narrow your team: Census Taker, Cartographer, Fortune Teller, Oracle, a working blocker. On the night that brings parity, kill a Sniper or Vigilante who still has shots. Remember that freshly claimed investigators are often guarded. (seen in 34 games)
- After a failed kill, assume a protector and remove them. Retry once if no Medic is plausible, since Narrow Escape works only once. If one holder's attacks keep failing on different targets, move the Katana: they may be Shaky. (seen in 33 games)
- Smuggler swaps: aim the Katana at a decoy and swap the decoy with your real target to beat a Medic's guard. Or swap yourself with a Villager on a night you expect a check or a shot. Swaps move Astral checks and your team's own actions too, so plan them in night chat. (seen in 5 games)

## Medic
- Guard the likeliest Katana target: loud analysts, then claimed investigators. Free Standard protection doesn't stop Strong attacks (SK, ignite), so spend tokens once a Neutral killer shows. Never visit a possible Veteran, and don't die holding tokens. (seen in 34 games)

## Vigilante
- Holding every shot makes you a Vanilla. Set a trigger by Day 2-3 and fire a 1-token Basic shot on a real lead while the Village still has numbers. Post a kill (and the role you learn) the next Day. (seen in 20 games)
