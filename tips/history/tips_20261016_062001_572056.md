# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-16 06:19 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 388 active entries of tips_notebook.json (95 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules 7c29fba5c41496e6.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of every nomination's Yes count, every trial total and each player's stated vote. When a stated vote doesn't fit a small total, it is usually a miscast. Ask that player once to re-check their reminder, and treat it as a lead, not grounds for a hang. (seen in 22 games)
- When a total doesn't fit, list the plain causes first: a miscast, a lie (Jesters, Hitmen, Arsonists and Turncoats all have reasons to lie) or a Merciful or Zealot trait. Ask voters to post how their vote counted. Assume a Fixer only once something shows one. A total above the number of voters means a Ballot Stuffer, after subtracting one vote for a revealed Mayor. If your own vote counted the opposite way, you are probably fixed for the Day. (seen in 10 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens on the first Day or night they can swing a vote or remove a threat, because they die with you. Kill tokens are the exception: they need a lead, meaning a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits. A claim clash or 'testing' an unverified claim is not a lead. At parity, fire anyway. (seen in 32 games)
- Answer a question about your night action plainly the first time it's asked. Repeated evasion becomes the case against you, even when the true answer is harmless. (seen in 27 games)
- A visit shows where someone went, not what they did there. A lone visitor to a Katana victim, confirmed by a second source, is near-proof. One unverified claimant's watch, map or count is only that claimant's word. (seen in 50 games)
- Surviving an attack confirms little. Serial Killers and Arsonists have Standard Defense, and Jesters and Executioners have Basic. Narrow Escape saves anyone once. A Shaky holder's Katana never kills. Paranoid fakes attack notices. A Villager's Stolen Blade attack reads exactly like the Katana. Ask the table who protected the survivor, and don't press the survivor to name a protector they can't know. (seen in 27 games)
- A Turncoat is a Villager who wins with the Yakuza. They read Evil to alignment checks, count as Good in a census, and show their real role to exact-role checks. Watch for a Village claimant who keeps protecting, defending and PMing the main suspect. Count them as a Yakuza vote when you work out how close the vote lock is. (seen in 2 games)

## Village
- Investigators: once a result bears on a vote, post the night, target and exact result. A result held back and revealed later looks retrofitted. If a Ritualist may be alive, a firm exact-role claim tends to get you killed that night, so put the full log in your will before you claim. (seen in 42 games)
- Don't announce tonight's target or say you'll act tonight, not even by PM. A Wiretap may read every PM. Do put tonight's plan in your will, with what each possible result would mean. (seen in 55 games)
- On Days 1-2, don't hint at a power role and don't lead the talk without a result. The organizer is the usual Night 1 Katana target. (seen in 41 games)
- Before voting Guilty, name a concrete, corroborated mechanical case: a Yakuza-only claim, a first-person slip, a broken claim or an elimination result. Results that look impossible often come from a Roleplayer. Discount those results rather than hanging the claimant. When the vote lock is one night away, hang the best case available. (seen in 76 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows someone is Yakuza, look next at whoever defended them, voted No or argued for delay, or PMed them. An Evil read alone isn't that kind of proof: Jesters, Executioners, Notorious Villagers and framed players read Evil too. (seen in 53 games)
- No role except Mayor, Vulture and Hitman is unique, so both claimants of a duplicate role can be real. Test both with checks; don't hang either one on the clash alone. (seen in 49 games)
- If a likely Jester must be hanged, keep the Guilty votes to a bare majority, and have confirmed power roles vote Innocent. The Jester's revenge kills a random Guilty voter. (seen in 27 games)
- Count the vote lock in effective votes: Yakuza plus votes that always count Innocent. Near the lock, give the hang to a Yakuza lead rather than a Neutral killer. (seen in 14 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers deliberately. Read every cause of death, keep a suspect list (unclaimed players, unexplained survivors, gaps in visit counts) and hang them while the table is still large. A game that continues after the Yakuza look dead means a Serial Killer, Arsonist, Werewolf or Vulture may be alive. (seen in 19 games)
- If you are Remorseful, you die if you vote Guilty on a Villager who is hanged. Vote Guilty only on corroborated cases and say so publicly. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village.

## Yakuza
- Don't defend a doomed teammate. Vote Yes and Guilty with the town, and say the vote you actually cast. A hidden Innocent shows up in the totals. (seen in 64 games)
- Don't PM teammates by Day. Everyone sees that a PM was sent, and PM triangles become the town's lead for the rest of the game. (seen in 71 games)
- Confirm the Katana holder and target in night chat every night, and every member should submit their vote. With two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 43 games)
- On Night 1, agree on a real Village cover for each member that fits their tokens and visits. Claims of Grunt, any other Yakuza-only role or Vanilla tend to be fatal, and so does switching claims once cornered. (seen in 26 games)
- Kill the players whose next result narrows your options first: Census Takers, counters and exact-role checkers. After a failed Katana attack, kill the likely protector instead of retrying the same target. (seen in 40 games)
- Don't deny a visit that a watch, map or count could expose. Admit it at once with the cover you agreed in night chat. (seen in 20 games)

## Coroner
- Examine bodies that settle a live question, such as a hanged suspect or one side of a claim duel, on the first night one exists. Post the night, target and role in one message. Don't spend a token on a cleaned body. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Protect the likeliest Katana targets, and never visit a possible Veteran. Free Standard protection doesn't stop Strong attacks (Serial Killer, ignite), so spend a token on Strong protection once a Neutral killer shows. (seen in 42 games)

## Vigilante
- Holding every shot wastes them. Fire a Basic shot on a real lead while the Village has numbers to spare. A Basic shot that fails fits innate Defense or Narrow Escape; shoot that player again later with 2 tokens. (seen in 27 games)
