# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-17 13:57 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 419 active entries of tips_notebook.json (101 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules 8f2138dae76eb0b8.

## Most often decisive
- Duplicate claims: only the Mayor, Vulture, Hitman and Saint are Unique. Two real holders tend to pick the same targets from the few unclaimed players, so a later claim that matches the first is not proof of copying. But 'roles can repeat' is not evidence for either claimant. Test both that Day or night (a watch, an exact-role check, a target with known visits) instead of hanging the later one on timing or leaving both untested. (seen in 52 games)
- Before you vote Guilty, name a corroborated mechanical break: a Yakuza-only role claim, a first-person slip about a night action, a knowledge leak, a corroborated lone visitor to a victim, or a group result whose other members are confirmed. A result from one unverified claimant (who may be a Roleplayer) usually breaks the result, not the player. Once one more night kill could bring the lock, hang the best case available that Day. If you're Remorseful, a Guilty vote on a hanged Villager kills you too, so claim the trait before the vote and vote Guilty only on such a break. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village, so look at who built that case. (seen in 81 games)
- A lone visitor to a Katana victim is near-proof only when it's corroborated: a second source, the named visitors confirming their visits, or the claimant's checked record. A single unverified watch, track or map is just the claimant's word, especially one that was held back or names the claimant's own accuser. Jesters and Yakuza invent exactly this pattern. (seen in 52 games)
- Yakuza: sending no daytime PMs between teammates, and none at all on a Day a teammate is under pressure, usually leaves the town's ledger with nothing on you. Everyone sees that a PM was sent, and a Wiretap or Eavesdropper may read it. Village: map PM clusters and ask both sides publicly what was said. (seen in 75 games)
- Yakuza: do the vote math in night chat before defending a doomed teammate. If your votes can't save them, vote Yes and Guilty with the town and state the vote you actually cast. Totals get compared with stated votes, and a hidden Innocent or a public defense gets pinned on you later. (seen in 68 games)

## Everyone
- Keep a notepad of every nomination and trial: each player's stated vote, the totals and your own vote. When a total doesn't fit, list the plain causes first. Most often it's a miscast or mispost. After that comes a lie (often from a Neutral or a Turncoat) or a hidden Merciful/Zealot trait. Ask that player once to re-check their reminder and post what they cast and how it counted. Don't hang on the mismatch alone. Assume a Fixer only once something shows one. Then one extra Guilty on a Village claimant's trial points at whoever pushed that hang. A total above the voter count means a revealed Mayor or a Ballot Stuffer. (seen in 24 games)
- Turncoat: a Villager who wins only with the Yakuza. They read Evil to a Sheriff but count Good in a census, and an exact-role check shows a real Village role. A claimant who keeps protecting and voting to save the main suspect is likely one: count them as a Yakuza vote in the lock math. (seen in 2 games)
- A survived Katana attack can come from a hidden Shaky holder (level None, never kills), the hidden Narrow Escape trait (once per player) or a Villager's Stolen Blade, not just a Medic or innate Defense. Failures on several targets fit one Shaky holder. Yakuza: move the Katana if one member's attacks keep failing. (seen in 3 games)
- Before each night, write tonight's target in your will, with what each result would mean. Post high-value results publicly while you're alive: the result from the night you die never reaches your will. (seen in 56 games)
- Answer a direct question about your night action in one plain sentence the first time it is asked. Evasion repeated over Days becomes the case against you. (seen in 29 games)

## Village
- Investigators: post a result that bears on a vote in your first speech after you get it, with night, target and exact wording. A result held back until someone counterclaims reads as retrofitted. When a Ritualist may be alive, give only as much of your role as the result needs, and put your full log in your will first. (seen in 47 games)
- Don't announce tonight's target or that you'll act tonight, in public or by PM. On Days 1 and 2, don't hint at a power role you have no result for: the hinter tends to be the Night 1 kill. (seen in 53 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows a player is Yakuza, the next leads are whoever argued No or delay on their nomination, defended them, or exchanged PMs with them. A caught liar or a lone Evil read may be a Jester, so check that first: did they self-nominate, ask for the rope, or push hangs with no data? (seen in 55 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers deliberately. Read every cause of death and keep a suspect list: unwatched no-action claimants, unexplained survivors, gaps in the visit counts. A Not Evil read doesn't clear them. Count down to the lock in effective votes: the Yakuza plus fixed Innocents (the nominee's own vote, a declared Merciful, a likely Turncoat). Near the lock the hang goes to your best Yakuza lead. The Day before the lock is your last real hang, so turn down 'one more night'. While the Katana still kills, don't spend a hang on a Neutral that never blocks wins. (seen in 22 games)

## Yakuza
- Agree each member's cover in Night 1 chat: a real Village role that fits your tokens and your real visits. Never claim Vanilla, Grunt or any Yakuza-only role. Check every claimed visit against the visit results already posted, and keep the claim fixed once made. Switching after a break is a case on its own. (seen in 35 games)
- Submit the agreed Katana holder, target and action every night. With two Yakuza, one missing vote cancels the kill. (seen in 44 games)
- Rank kills by what the victim will still produce: a Census Taker first, then group and count roles (Scout, Cartographer, Fortune Teller), a working blocker, and exact-role checkers. After a failed attack, deal with the likely protector rather than retrying blindly. (seen in 42 games)

## Neutral
- Jesters and Executioners: a Sheriff's Evil read on you is often the town's only hard fact, while an open Jester claim or repeated self-nominations make the town refuse to hang you. A committed, Village-sounding case tends to work better. (seen in 9 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil, so a clean read on a suspected attacker clears nothing. Say so when you post it. Still rank unchecked players above clean ones while several Yakuza may be alive. (seen in 29 games)

## Coroner
- Examine the body that settles a live question (one side of a duplicate claim, a hanged suspect) on the first night it exists. Post night, target and role in one message. Don't spend a token on a body cleaned by a Mortician, and remember that a Roleplayer shows as 'Roleplayer'. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Protect vocal analysts early and claimed investigators later, and vary the pattern. Never protect a possible Veteran. Once a Strong attacker shows up (a Serial Killer cause, an ignite), spend tokens on Strong protection. Dying with tokens unspent wastes them. (seen in 43 games)

## Vigilante
- Holding every shot makes you a Vanilla Villager. Fire a Basic shot on a real lead, meaning a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits, while the Village still has numbers to spare. Don't fire on a bare claim clash or to 'test' a claimed Medic. If it kills, post the night, the target and the role you learned. (seen in 30 games)
