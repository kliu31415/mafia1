# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-15 08:09 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 380 active entries of tips_notebook.json (90 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules db4418999fb3afd9.

## Everyone
- Ballots are secret, so keep a notepad of every stated vote, every announced total and every role claim, by Day. A stated vote that doesn't fit a small total is usually a miscast. Ask the player once to re-check, and don't hang on the mismatch alone. (seen in 38 games)
- If a total still doesn't fit after voters re-check how their vote counted (a Zealot's always counts Guilty, a Merciful's always Innocent), someone manipulated it. More votes than voters means a Ballot Stuffer, once you allow for a revealed Mayor's double vote. One extra Guilty on a Village claimant's trial usually means a Fixer wanted that hang, so look at who pushed it. (seen in 10 games)
- Tokens die with you, so spend one-shot tokens as soon as they can swing a vote or stop a threat. Fire kill tokens on a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits. A claim clash or a 'test' of an unverified claim isn't enough. Once the Yakuza hold half the votes, it usually pays to fire even on a coin flip. (seen in 30 games)
- Each night, write tonight's planned target in your will, and what each possible result would mean. A result from the night you die never reaches your will, so post results that matter to a vote while you're alive. (seen in 54 games)
- Only the Vulture is Unique, so two players claiming the same role are often both real. Test both claims with watches, exact-role checks, or whether a result came out before the facts it shows were public. Don't judge them by tone. (seen in 48 games)
- A lone visitor to a Katana victim is close to proof when a second source backs the report. When the report comes from one unverified claimant, it is only their word. (seen in 47 games)
- When someone survives an attack and nobody says they protected them, the cause may be innate Defense (Serial Killer, Arsonist), the hidden Narrow Escape trait, a Shaky Katana holder whose attack can't kill, or a fake notice from the Paranoid trait. A Villager's Stolen Blade attack also reads 'Katana'. Failed attacks on several different targets suggest a Shaky holder. A survival that the player confirms while claiming a role with no Defense fits a Neutral killer. (seen in 26 games)
- Answer questions about your night action in one plain sentence the first time you're asked. Evasion repeated over Days becomes the case against you. (seen in 24 games)

## Village
- Vote Guilty on a mechanical case, such as:
- a claim of a Yakuza-only role
- a first-person slip or a knowledge leak
- a corroborated lone visit to a victim
- a group result whose other members are confirmed
- a broken claim.
Echoed leans and wording aren't cases. But on the Day before the vote lock, hang the best case you have. (seen in 71 games)
- Post the vote-lock count each morning. Once the Yakuza hold half the living players, no nomination can pass: with 2 Yakuza that happens at 4 alive, with 3 Yakuza at 6. Assume every Yakuza is still alive unless something showed a hanged player was Yakuza. (seen in 13 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows a player is Yakuza, look next at whoever argued for delay, defended them or PM'd them. A player who is merely caught lying, or read Evil once, may be a Jester, an Executioner or framed. (seen in 50 games)
- Investigators: post the night, target and exact result as soon as it bears on a vote. Don't announce tonight's target, in public or by PM, since a Wiretap may read PMs. While a Ritualist may be alive, claiming your exact role without a result that changes a vote tends to get you killed. (seen in 50 games)
- On Days 1-2, avoid hinting at a power role, and avoid leading loudly when you have no result. The most leader-like voice is often the Night 1 kill. (seen in 39 games)
- Run the Jester check: an Evil read on a player who self-nominates, claims nothing or says 'judge me' fits a Jester. It's usually better to leave them alive. If you do hang them, keep the Guilty votes to a bare majority, with confirmed roles voting Innocent. (seen in 27 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers on purpose, and read every cause of death. A Serial Killer death, or a night with more deaths than the Katana explains, proves a Neutral killer is alive. A 'hidden gun' death shows no side. Keep a list of no-action claimants nobody has watched and unexplained survivors. A 'Vanilla' claim is a lie unless a hand-picked list names that role. Hang your best suspect while the table is still large. Near the lock, spend the hang on a Yakuza lead instead. (seen in 18 games)
- There is at most one Turncoat: a Villager who wins only if the Yakuza win. They read Evil to a Sheriff or Seer but count as Good in a census. An Evil read plus a Good census count fits a Turncoat or a Notorious Villager. Hanging a Turncoat doesn't lower the Yakuza count.
- A Remorseful player who votes Guilty dies if the hanged player was truly Village. If you're Remorseful, say so and vote Innocent on thin hangs. A 'remorse' death proves the hanged player was Village, so look at who built that case.

## Yakuza
- Don't send teammates PMs during the Day, and on Days when a teammate is under pressure send no PMs at all. A visible cluster of PMs becomes the town's lead for the rest of the game. (seen in 68 games)
- Don't defend a teammate who is doomed. Vote Yes and Guilty with the town and say so, because the announced totals expose hidden Innocent votes. (seen in 60 games)
- In Night 1 chat, agree on Village cover roles that fit each member's tokens and visits. Avoid Yakuza-only roles and Vanilla Villager. Confirm the Katana holder and the target every night. (seen in 41 games)
- Kill the player whose next result would narrow down your team, such as a Census Taker, Cartographer or Lookout. After a failed attack, assume the target was protected or has Defense, and remove the protector rather than attacking the same target again. (seen in 36 games)

## Coroner
- On the first night the body exists, examine the one that settles a live question, such as one side of a duplicate claim or a hanged suspect. Post the night, target and role the next Day, and don't announce your next exam. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Protect the likeliest Katana targets, but never a player who may be a Veteran: an alerting Veteran attacks every visitor, you included. Free Standard protection doesn't stop Strong attacks (a Serial Killer, an Arsonist's ignite), so spend tokens once a Neutral killer shows up. (seen in 37 games)
