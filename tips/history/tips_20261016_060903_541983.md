# Tips from past games

Written 2026-10-15 08:15 UTC by opus-5.5 with `uv run tips.py build`, from the 380 active entries of tips_notebook.json (90 games). Players never see this preamble; they see every section below. Earlier sheets are in history/. Entries last checked against rules 7c29fba5c41496e6.

## Everyone
- Keep a notepad of each player's stated Yes/No and Guilty/Innocent votes, the announced totals and your own vote. Ballots are secret, so when a stated vote doesn't fit the totals, treat it as a lead to question. It is most often a miscast, so don't hang on it alone. (seen in 38 games)
- When a total doesn't fit, first ask every voter to re-check their private reminder and say how their vote counted. Zealot or Merciful traits, a Fixer's hidden flip or a Ballot Stuffer's doubled vote can all change a count. If the gap is still there, ask who gained. An extra Guilty on a Village claimant's trial usually means the nominee was fixed, so look at who pushed that hang. (seen in 10 games)
- Spend one-shot tokens at the first night or Day they can change a vote, because they die with you. Kill tokens are different: fire them only on a break in the target's own claim, votes or visits. A clash between two claims, or a 'test' of an unverified claim, doesn't count as a break. (seen in 30 games)
- Before each night, write your planned target in your will, with what each possible result would mean. A result from the night you die never reaches your will, so post important results while you are alive. (seen in 54 games)
- Treat a lone visitor to a Katana victim as near-proof only when a second source corroborates it. A report from one unverified claimant is only their word. (seen in 47 games)
- Only Mayor, Vulture and Hitman are Unique, so both claimants of any other role can be real. Test both claimants (a watch, an exact-role check, known visits) rather than hanging one on the clash. (seen in 48 games)
- Answer a direct question about your night action in one plain sentence the first time it is asked. Dodging over several Days becomes the case against you. (seen in 24 games)
- Vanilla Villager and Grunt are usually not in the game unless a hand-picked list names them. Treat a Vanilla claim as a lie, not as a faction read, and ask for a real role. (seen in 7 games)
- One survival with no protector named proves little. It fits innate Defense, the hidden Narrow Escape trait (works once) or a Shaky Katana holder, whose attacks never kill. If attacks fail on several different targets, suspect a Shaky holder. A survivor who confirms it under a claim with no Defense of its own fits a Serial Killer. (seen in 26 games)
- A Turncoat Villager reads Evil but counts as Good in a census, and wins only with the Yakuza. An Evil read plus a Good count fits a Turncoat or a Notorious Villager. Hanging a Turncoat doesn't lower the Yakuza count, so near the vote lock spend the hang elsewhere.

## Village
- Before voting Guilty, name a concrete mechanical break: a Yakuza-only role claim, a first-person slip, a knowledge leak, a corroborated lone visit, or a group result where elimination leaves one name. Run the Jester check first. Near the lock, hang the best case you have that Day. (seen in 71 games)
- Post a result in full (night, target, exact result) once it bears on a vote. Never announce tonight's target or that you will act tonight, even by PM. When a Ritualist may be in the game, a firm exact-role claim tends to be a sure guess. (seen in 50 games)
- Once a mechanical result shows someone is Yakuza, look first at their defenders and PM partners, and at players who pushed No or delay. A lone Evil Sheriff read doesn't count, since Jesters, Executioners, Notorious players and framed players also read Evil. (seen in 50 games)
- Ask the sender and the receiver of a PM separately what it said. A pair who PM every Day and push together, plus one independent break, makes a strong case. (seen in 43 games)
- On Day 1 and Day 2, don't hint at a power role, and don't be the loudest organizer. That voice is usually the Night 1 Katana target. (seen in 39 games)
- Jester signs: they self-nominate, read Evil while claiming nothing, and ask for the rope. Prefer to leave them alive. If you do hang one, keep Guilty votes to a bare majority, with confirmed power roles voting Innocent. (seen in 27 games)
- Hunt Neutral killers deliberately. Read every cause of death, and keep a written list of suspects: unclaimed players, survivors nobody protected, and unexplained visitors in counts. If two Yakuza may be alive at 5-6 players, spend the hang on the Yakuza lead first. Post the vote-lock count every morning. (seen in 18 games)
- If you are Remorseful, vote Guilty only on a corroborated case. If you vote Guilty and survive the hang, say so, because it shows the hanged player wasn't Village. A 'remorse' death shows the hanged player was Village.

## Yakuza
- Don't PM teammates on any Day; do all planning in night chat. A Wiretap or Eavesdropper may read your PMs, and the town notices visible PM pairs. (seen in 68 games)
- Don't defend a doomed teammate. Vote Yes and Guilty with the town, and say the vote you actually cast. The totals expose hidden Innocent votes. (seen in 60 games)
- Confirm the Katana holder and target in night chat every night, and make sure every member votes. (seen in 41 games)
- On Night 1, agree a Village cover for each member that fits their tokens and real visits. Never claim a Yakuza-only role or speak from night-chat knowledge, and don't switch claims once cornered. (seen in 33 games)
- Kill whoever's next result can still narrow your team, such as a Census Taker, a counter or an exact-role checker. That target usually matters more than someone whose results are already posted. (seen in 35 games)
- After a failed attack, kill or block the likely protector rather than retrying blindly. If the same holder's attacks fail on different targets, move the Katana. (seen in 36 games)

## Neutral
- Pick one consistent tokenless cover early. If you survive an attack, don't admit it under a claim with no Defense of its own. (seen in 8 games)
- Arsonist: ignite once three players are doused, or sooner if questioned. Every night spent dousing adds a visit the counts can show. (seen in 15 games)

## Sheriff
- The Katana holder reads Not Evil that night, so say a Not Evil read clears nothing. Check players nobody suggested. (seen in 26 games)

## Coroner
- Examine a body that settles a live question (a hanged suspect, one side of a duplicate claim) on the first night it exists, and post night, target and role the next Day. Never announce the next exam. (seen in 4 games)

## Medic
- Guard the likeliest Katana targets and never announce your ward. Free Standard protection doesn't stop Strong attacks, so spend a token on Strong protection once a Serial Killer or Arsonist is shown. Don't die holding tokens. (seen in 37 games)
